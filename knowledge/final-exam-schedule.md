# Final Exam Schedule

The University requires that every class include a final exam except certain activity classes for which the dean of the college authorizes an exception. No instructor may change the scheduled time for a final exam without authorization from the dean of the college. Always check the final exam schedule before you register for your classes.

* * *

## Fall 2026 Schedule 

**Final Exams are based on the class start time** **with the exception of the following:**

-   **Saturday Classes:**
    Final exams for Saturday classes are scheduled Saturday, December 19 at the class start time.
-   **Combined Final Exams - CHEM 223A and 223B:**
    Final exams for all sections are scheduled Friday, December 18 from 5:00 to 7:00 p.m.

**Fall Final Exam Days are Monday through Saturday, December 14–19, 2026.**
