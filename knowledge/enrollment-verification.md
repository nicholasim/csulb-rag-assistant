# FAQs for Enrollment Verification vs. Official Transcripts

## What is a degree/enrollment verification?

A verification is a request for a university to verify a student's degree and enrollment information. Unlike a transcript, a verification is simply a confirmation of degrees earned and does not include course grades.

## Why would I need a degree/enrollment verification?

Students may need certification of enrollment to defer student loans, to provide proof of enrollment for their health insurance, to verify degrees for prospective employers or to show proof of academic standing to enroll in another institution.

## How do I verify degree and attendance online?

[**Verify your degrees and attendance online**](http://www.studentclearinghouse.org/) if you are a current or former student of the university.

## My loan provider is requesting a letter of enrollment status. What do I do?

If your student loan servicer is requesting your enrollment status, inform them that the information is available at the [**National Student Clearinghouse**](http://www.studentclearinghouse.org/)**.**

## How can a third party request student verification?

The University participates in the [**National Student Clearinghouse**](http://www.studentclearinghouse.org/) Core Service for deferment of student loans and verification of degrees. Employers and background screening firms should contact the National Student Clearinghouse to request [**printed verification of enrollment and degrees**](http://www.studentclearinghouse.org/)**.**

If you have a FERPA block in place or a University Financial Hold, information is withheld from the third parties by the National Student Clearinghouse. If a third party requires more detailed academic information, you may need to request an official transcript.

## Why was my degree/enrollment unable to be verified?

Occasionally, verifications cannot be completed for the following reasons:

-   If you have a FERPA block in place or have a University Financial Hold, information protected under FERPA is withheld from the third parties by the National Student Clearinghouse.
-   Not enough information was provided for a positive identification
-   Conflicting Information (e.g., former name)
-   Specialized Certificate Program

In the event of an incomplete verification, the third party may choose to submit another request and include additional information.

## What is a transcript?

A transcript is a comprehensive record of academic information that includes coursework, grades, credit hours, GPA, and degrees earned.  Unofficial transcripts are free and are available via [MyCSULB](https://my.csulb.edu/).  Official transcripts bear the official seal of the University and are required in some situations**.**

## How can I get an unofficial transcript?

This is a free service to currently enrolled students. Log in to your [MyCSULB](https://my.csulb.edu/) account and in the drop-down Other Academic menu, select Unofficial Transcripts. Students may request an unofficial transcript when they wish to review their entire academic history including all courses and grades taken at CSULB as well as transfer courses and grades.  In addition, the unofficial transcript provides term and cumulative GPA. 

## When do I need an official transcript?

Students would request an official transcript when they are transferring to another institution, applying to graduate school, applying for an internship or applying for a scholarship.

## How can I get an official transcript?

For currently enrolled students who require an Official Transcript for college application, college transfer, internship, or scholarship opportunities, go to your [MyCSULB](https://my.csulb.edu/) account and in the drop-down Other Academic menu, select Official Transcripts. This will bring you to the Credential Solutions Self-Service Site through a secure connection. When finished, close the Credential window and return to the [MyCSULB](https://my.csulb.edu/) page.

For alumni, [click here.](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/transcripts-former-studentsalumni)

## Can I request an electronic pdf transcript via email?

Yes, when you place your order for an official transcript, an electronic option is available.
