# Resource Centers

## Cultural Resource Centers

## Asian, Pacific Islander, and Desi Resource Center

The mission of the [**Asian, Pacific Islander, and Desi Resource Center**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/asian-pacific-islander-desi-resource-center "Asian, Pacific Islander, & Desi Resource Center") is to cultivate an environment and programming dedicated to the support, belonging, and development of Asian, Pacific Islander, and Desi (APID) students. We strive to create a cohesive APID community while uplifting historically marginalized and currently underrepresented APID student groups on campus.

-   **Location:** FO4-276 

## Black Resource Center

The mission of the [**Black Resource Center**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/black-resource-center) is to provide a facility for Black/African students, staff and faculty to come together in a secure, supportive, well maintained, functional, and Black/African centered environment. The focus of the center is to benefit the cultural growth and development of the CSULB Black/African community.

-   **Location:** FO4-274

 

## LGBTQ+ Resource Center

The [**LGBTQ+ Resource Center**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/lgbtq-resource-center "LGBTQ+ Resource Center") was established to provide support services for students.  The Center hopes to advocate for and educate on topics related to homophobia, heterosexism, inclusive language, and other sexual and gender identity issues. The center's space can be used for group meetings, support programs, teach-ins, and other sexual and gender identity-related programs.

-   **Location:** FO4-165
     

## Muslim Resource Center

The Muslim Resource Centers promotes the faith formation of the Muslim students on campus while supporting their academic, social, and professional goals. The goal of the center is to provide a home away from home for those seeking to engage in the Muslim traditions.

-   **Location:** FO4-282
     

## Puvungna Resource Center

The Puvungna Resource Center serves to create an atmosphere of special support, cultural enrichment, academic retention, and most importantly, a sense of community for Native American/American Indian students. The center operates to provide academic and cultural resources for the CSULB campus community.

-   **Location:** University Student Union (USU), Suite 310
     

## Nuestro Centro Latine Resource Center

The [**Latine** **Resource Center's**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/latine-resource-center "Latine Resource Center") goal is to provide resources that educate students about the many historical and cultural backgrounds that make up the Chicanx & Latine population. This also includes community organizing and the promotion and education of social movements and political issues that continue to affect Chicanxs and Latine today.

-   **Location:** FO4-262

_All students, regardless of sex, race, color, national origin, or any other protected identity, who are interested in the program’s mission and focus, are welcome and eligible to participate in all Office of Belonging & Inclusion (OBI) events and programs._
