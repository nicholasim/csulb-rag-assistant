# CSULB Computer Labs

CSULB hosts a variety of labs on campus that can be utilized by all students.  Expand the menus below to find details and locations.

## Open Campus Labs

These labs are open to all students regardless of major / field of study.

## Horn Center Open Computer Lab

**Location**: South Campus \[[map](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!m/413927?ce/41627?bm/?ct/45996,44104,41975,55572,55573,58407)\]

[The Horn Center Open Computer Lab](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-technology-services/horn-center) provides open access to PCs and Macs loaded with campus-licensed software, docking stations for laptops, collaboration/group meeting tables, study tables equipped with charging connections, and the Technology Help Desk for onsite technical assistance.

The lab is open to all CSULB students. Students may enter the lab and access any workstation at their convenience for any amount of time. 

## Spidell Technology Center, University Library

**Location**: Spidell Technology Center, 1st Floor, University Library \[[map](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!m/562212?ct/45996,44104,41975,55572,55573)\]

[The Spidell Technology Center](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/services/how-to/computer-labs-at-the-university-library) is open to all CSULB students and provides access to PCS and Macs. There is an automated sign-up that assigns computers to users as workstations become available during busy periods. Computers are loaded with software, and some stations are especially set up for use with Zoom. The lab is available whenever the [library is open](https://csulb.libcal.com/).

## University Express Lab

**Location**: Shakarian Student Success Center, Room 20 \[[map](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!m/412216?ct/45996,44104,41975,55572,55573)\]

[The University Express Lab](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-technology-services/classroom-support-services/university-express-lab) at the Student Success Center gives students quick access to computers and printers in a central campus location. The equipment includes 9 PC Workstations, 3 Apple iMacs, 2 Black White Printers.

## Remote and Virtual Labs

With access to remote labs, students can work with software not available on their personal computers. Some applications may not work as intended due to the limitations of remote desktops. 

## Academic Department Remote Labs

Remote labs may be available in certain departments so you can work with specific software needed for your program. A VPN connection is needed before accessing the remote labs:  [Remote Access Service](https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/Requests/ServiceDet?ID=28159)

Visit [Student Remote Labs](http://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=114096) for more information.

## Student Virtual Lab

In the [CSULB Student Virtual Lab (SVL)](https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/Requests/ServiceDet?ID=28316), students can conveniently utilize university-supported software applications and enhance their computing power at no extra cost. 

The SVL is available 24/7 from any remote location.

-   To access, visit [Single Sign-On](http://www.sso.csulb.edu/) and select the SVL app.
-   [Visit the SVL](https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/Requests/ServiceDet?ID=28316) support site for a listing of software available.

## Campus Wireless Printing

Students who are physically present on campus can use the [wireless printing network](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-technology-services/wireless-printing) to send files to print at the campus locations listed below. 

**How to Pay**
To pay for print jobs at these locations, you'll need your student ID card with money (Beach Bucks) loaded on it. Visit [How to Add Money](https://www.csulb.edu/49er-shops-at-the-beach/how-to-add-money) for more information.

-   Horn Center Computer Lab
-   Library Spidell Center
-   College of Business Open Access Lab
-   Beachside College Lab
-   College of Engineering
-   University Bookstore
