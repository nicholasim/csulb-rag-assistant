## Welcome!

The **University Writing Center** is a campus-wide writing support service for all currently enrolled CSULB students. We support all students, across all disciplines, and of all class standings. **Our goal is to help** _**you**_ **become an effective and independent writer.** 

## Who We Are

Consider the UWC as “all things writing.” 

We cover all writing tutorial services for our campus community, including: 

## All Students  

This includes students who are undergraduates, graduates, in the credential program, or in other professional certificate programs. If you are a **current** CSULB student, we can help you.

## All Majors 

We can service all majors on campus with their writing endeavors. In our tutorial sessions, we prioritize high-order concerns in writing, including meta-organization and clarity, development, cohesiveness, audience, and – when applicable – thesis strength.  

Another key focus in tutorial sessions is adhering to writing expectations in your field. The “qualities of writing” that a discipline values can vary greatly. It’s our priority to ensure students understand the genre they are writing within.

## All Types of Writing 

This includes academic writing assignments, personal projects, and any writing-related endeavor you may pursue during your educational journey at CSULB.  

-   _Academic_: We can assist students with various academic assignments, from traditional essays and research papers, to proposals, presentations, thesis and dissertation projects, discussion board posts, professional emails, and more.  

-   _Professional_: We can assist students with the development of professional materials, including application materials, personal statements, cover letters, resumes, curriculum vitaes (CV), statement of purpose, and more.  

-   _Personal_: We can assist with any personal writing projects, including column and article development for personal publication, poetry, short story, novel development, other creative writing pursuits, website writing, blogging, social media writing, and more.  

## Any Stage of Writing 

Here, we prioritize the writing “process” over the “product,” meaning that our resource values the time and effort that contribute to the writing process, rather than focusing on a “final” product.  

With this mindset, our sessions aim to meet students where they’re at during the writing process. Our tutors can assist with:  

-   _Preparation_ 

-   _Prewriting_

-   _Brainstorming_ 

-   _Development_  

-   _Organization_ 

-   _Drafting_ 

-   _Revising_ 

-   _Proofreading_ 

-   _Formatting_ 

-   _& More!_ 

## What We Do

The UWC offers individualized writing instruction (tutoring) to CSULB students in one-hour tutorial sessions, hosted by professionally trained peer-tutors. We service both novice and experienced writers at the undergraduate and graduate levels to support students in the development of guided, independent writing skills.  

In tutorial sessions, our center offers appointments in a one-on-one format, group sessions, and diverse modalities – including in-person appointments and online appointments (via Zoom). 

Beyond tutorial services, the UWC is actively involved in our campus community, including:  

## Workshops 

We host weekly workshops online (via Zoom), featuring select writing-related topics. All CSULB students are welcome to register and join workshops.

For more information, please visit our Workshop page.  

## Outreach 

We are avid supporters of CSULB students, faculty, and staff.

In our outreach efforts, we offer classroom visits where we share more information about UWC services with students; we visit department meetings to connect with faculty; and we engage with administrators to build an impactful presence on campus.  

## Events  

We attend a wide array of events on campus, from orientations to topic-based events, to campus-wide tabling opportunities.

To review the events we will attend this semester, please visit the Events page.  

## Partnerships 

We value campus partnerships and relationship-building with other resources on campus to help serve the greatest number of CSULB students. 

Some of our partnerships include our involvement in Beach XP, Bridge to the Beach, the American Language Institute (ALI), #TheBeachWrites, the College of Education Student Resource Center, the College of Business Summer Bootcamp, the McNair Program, and more. 

If you are a campus partner and you are interested in collaborating with the UWC, please visit our Contact page. 

## Why Us

Our center offers a versatile tutorial service that takes a contextual and holistic approach to developing successful writing skills. With our professionally trained staff and ability to meet students’ needs, we offer a valuable source of writing support for our campus community.  

The UWC’s diverse flexibility and approaches to writing creates a welcoming tutorial environment for all students who visit.
