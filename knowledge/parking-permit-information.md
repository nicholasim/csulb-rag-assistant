# Permit Information & Regulations

Delay your parking permit purchase until after the first 4 weeks of classes and purchase a [Long Beach Transit bus pass](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/go-beach-discounted-bus-pass) instead, and you will receive a free 30-day bus pass and 40% your semester parking permit with the new [First Month Free (FMF) program](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/first-month-free-30-day-bus-pass-discounted-parking-permit).

Consider not driving to campus at all by using the free [BeachGO app](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/beachgo-mobile-app) in combination with one of the many discounted forms of  [Sustainable Transportation](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/sustainable-transportation) as an alternative to driving and purchasing a parking permit.

CSULB uses [License Plate Recognition (LPR)](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/article/license-plate-recognition-lpr-virtual-permitting), a virtual permitting technology. You will not receive a physical permit after purchase. Your license plate will function as your parking permit.

## Important Regulations

-   Paid parking is required at all times.
-   Parking is enforced 24 hours a day, 7 days a week including holidays.
-   One primary vehicle is allowed per student parking permit, but this vehicle can be changed at any time (i.e., rental car or loaner vehicle).
-   Only one active permit can be purchased at a time. Individuals will be cited for using two separate permit types at the same time.
-   All permits are non-transferable and can only be used by the individual who purchased them. Parking privileges may be revoked for individuals found sharing permits or falsifying a permit.
-   Each permit has its own parking location privileges on campus (please see below for information on where your permit is allowed).

-   Employees who are employed at two or more CSU campuses are only required to purchase a single parking permit.

### Employee Carpool Program

[BeachGO](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/beachgo-mobile-app) is a free mobile app exclusive to CSULB that can help you match with other employees who wish to carpool to campus. ### Daily Parking

-   Daily permits are valid in any General (G) Lots as well as all three (3) campus parking structures unless the space is otherwise restricted with posted signage (ADA, State Vehicle, Loading/Unloading, etc.).
-   Once you purchase a Daily Permit, you may move your vehicle to any location on campus where Daily Permits are valid. You do not have to remain only in the lot where your permit was purchased.
-   Daily permits expire on campus at 11:59 p.m. on the date of purchase.
-   Daily permits are also valid in Employee (E) Lots after 5:30 p.m., Monday-Friday, and all day on weekends.

### Evening/Weekend

-   Evening/Weekend permits are available after 5:30 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends for $10.
-   Evening/Weekend permits may be purchased at campus [pay stations](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/37105?ct/63337,45996,44104,41975,55572,55573,44735?s/) or on the [ParkMobile App](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/article/parkmobile-app).

-   Short-Term permits are required to park in [Short Term spaces](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/37105?ct/63337,45996,44104,41975,55572,55573,42039?s/).
-   Other Permit Types are not valid in Short-Term Spaces.
-   Short-Term parking is available in 30-, 60-, or 90-minute increments and can be purchased at the pay stations located within the parking lots where Short-Term spaces are located. -   Short-Term spaces are Green painted curbs.
-   Short-Term permits are valid only within the lot in which they are purchased. You cannot move to another short-term parking location with this permit.

## Accessible Parking

Students with a DMV-issued placard or distinguishing license plate and a valid parking permit may park in ADA spaces in General Lots, ADA spaces in Employee Lots, and any Short-Term parking space.

Please note, students with an ADA placard are not allowed to park in the regular Employee spaces unless it is after 5:30 p.m., Mon.-Fri. or all day on the weekends.

Parking permits (monthly, semester, academic year) are available for purchase online from the [Online Parking Portal](https://csulb.t2hosted.com/Account/Portal "Parking Portal"). Daily permits are available for purchase from a pay station or via the [ParkMobile App](http://us.parkmobile.com/mobile-apps). Students requesting specific parking accommodations should contact the Bob Murphy Access Center at 562.985.5401 or [BMAC@csulb.edu](mailto:BMAC@csulb.edu).

## Overnight Parking

Non-resident permits are required to depart the campus by 11:59 p.m. unless a special accommodation has been requested (see below). This includes Daily, Monthly, Semester, Academic Year and Employee permits. Student Resident permits for those living in campus residence halls are the only permit type on our campus that are allowed to park overnight.

### Housing Guests

All guests must purchase some form of paid parking to avoid citation. A Daily parking permit is valid in any General (G) Lot as well as all three campus parking structures unless the space is otherwise restricted with posted signage. Daily permits are also valid in Employee (E) Lot parking spaces after 5:30 p.m., Monday-Friday, and all day Saturday-Sunday.  Daily permits are not valid in [Resident (R) Lots](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/37105?ct/42099?s/) or the [Beachside Lot](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/42099?m/573658?s/). 

Overnight parking for guests of campus residents on the main campus is available for purchase in [Lot G2](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/41596?m/412286?s/) only. Overnight parking is valid 24 hours from the time of purchase. 

Overnight parking for the guests of residents at the Beachside Campus is available for purchase at the [Beachside Lot](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/42099?m/573658?s/) and is valid in the Short-Term parking spaces of that lot only. Overnight parking is available for purchase beginning at 5:30 p.m. and valid through 9:30 a.m. the following day. 

### Other overnight parking requests

Overnight guest parking is allowed in [Lot G2](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/41596?m/412286?s/) only. Overnight parking can be purchased from the pay station in either 24-, 48- and 72-hour increments. The time of purchase will mark the beginning of the 24-hour period.

Students or Employees with a valid permit (Semester, Monthly, or Academic Year) do not need to pay an additional overnight parking fee but rather must request approval by sending an email to [OvernightParkingRequests@csulb.edu](mailto:OvernightParkingRequests@csulb.edu). The request must be submitted 48 hours in advance. Please include the following information in your request:

1.  Name
2.  Date/time requested
3.  Campus ID Number
4.  License plate number
5.  Reason overnight parking is required (campus-related business, sporting event, field trip, etc.)

A confirmation response will be provided once your request has been approved.
