# Financial

Learn about our emergency grants and financial wellness assistance, providing crucial support for students facing unexpected financial challenges. 

* * *

## Emergency Grants

Emergency grants for sudden, unexpected expenses that are not included in financial aid support. Examples: Hospitalization or unexpected medical bills, emergency dental or medical procedures, sudden loss of childcare or childcare funds, sudden loss of transportation to job, campus or internship. Based on documentation of need. 

[Speak with a Case Manager about Emergency Grants](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/basic-needs/financial#)

* * *

## CSULB Virtual Financial Wellness Center

Explore CSULB's Financial Wellness Center! Our virtual wellness center is tailored to support students in building strong financial habits and making informed financial decisions during their college years and beyond. Financial Literacy 101 is FREE for life and offers practical advice and tools on budgeting, managing student loans, understanding credit, and making informed financial decisions to set students up for long-term success.

[Visit the Virtual Financial Wellness Center](https://csulb.financialliteracy101.org/public/welcome.cfm?code=CSULBSSS)
