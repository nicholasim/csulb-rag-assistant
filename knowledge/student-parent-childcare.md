# Childcare

Access to reliable childcare is an important part of student parent success. The following on-campus and off-campus childcare resources may help support your academic journey while ensuring your children are in a safe and nurturing environment. We encourage students to explore each option and connect directly with providers to learn more about services, availability, and eligibility requirements.

* * *

## On-Campus Services and Programs for Students with Children

## Isabel Patterson Child Development Center (IPCDC)

The Isabel Patterson Child Development Center (IPCDC) provides early childhood education and care for children of CSULB students, faculty, and staff. The center focuses on creating a safe, inclusive, and developmentally supportive environment where children can learn, grow, and thrive. Programs are designed to support working and student families while promoting children’s social, emotional, and academic development.

**Location:** 5700 Atherton Street, Long Beach, CA 90815

Students are encouraged to contact IPCDC directly for current enrollment information, program availability, waitlists, hours of operation, and eligibility details.

[Visit the IPCDC Website](https://www.asicsulb.org/corporate/discover/child-development-center)

## Child and Family Center (CFC)

The Child and Family Center (CFC) provides high-quality, NAEYC-accredited childcare and early education for children aged 3 months through preschool, serving CSULB students, faculty, staff, and Long Beach families. The center offers a safe, inclusive, and developmentally appropriate learning environment that supports children’s growth while empowering families navigating school, work, and parenting.

As a teaching and learning lab, the CFC also trains CSULB Child and Family Studies students and future early childhood professionals, ensuring children receive individualized attention and benefit from research-based, anti-bias educational practices. Through mentorship, family support, and early learning opportunities, the center fosters a strong foundation for lifelong learning and community connection.

[Visit the CFC Website](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-health-human-services/child-and-family-center)

## Off-Campus Services and Programs for Students with Children

The following resources are community-based organizations that offer childcare services and family support programs in Long Beach and surrounding areas. These programs are not affiliated with or endorsed by CSULB. Please contact each organization directly for the most up-to-date information regarding eligibility, fees, programming availability, and enrollment processes.

## Child Lane

[Child Lane](https://www.childlane.org/early-care-and-education) Provides early childhood education and comprehensive family support services, including childcare programs that promote school readiness and healthy development.

## Crystal Stairs

[Crystal Stairs](https://www.crystalstairs.org/) Offers childcare referrals, financial assistance programs, and support services to help families access quality childcare throughout Los Angeles County.

## Children’s Home Society

[Children's Home Society](https://chs-ca.org/) provides early education programs, family resources, and support services focused on strengthening families and supporting children’s development and well-being.

## KinderCare

[KinderCare](https://www.kindercare.com/about-us/who-we-are/our-approach-to-education) is a national childcare provider offering early childhood education programs for infants through school-age children at multiple locations.

## Precious Lamb

[Precious Lamb](https://preciouslamb.org/) provides childcare and early education programs with an emphasis on supporting families experiencing housing insecurity and economic hardship.
