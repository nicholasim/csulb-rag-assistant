# Schedule of Classes

**Understanding Modes of Instruction:** CSULB classes may be listed on the schedule as having one or more of the following formats available:

## Modes of In-Person Instruction

-   **Face-to-face on-campus**: in-person class meetings at scheduled days and times on campus
-   **Face-to-face off-campus:** in-person class meetings at scheduled days and times at a community site
-   **Hybrid with synchronous online:** some face-to-face instruction and some synchronous (scheduled days and times) online class meeting
-   **Hybrid with asynchronous online:** some face-to-face instruction and some asynchronous (no scheduled meeting times) online components
-   **Hyflex:** enrolled in face-to-face instruction, meeting at scheduled days and times

_**Please note that camera use may be required for synchronous and/or mixed online classes.

## Modes of Online-Only Instruction

-   **Synchronous online:** all classes will be remote at scheduled days and times
-   **Asynchronous online:** no scheduled day and time, so students can complete work at different times throughout the week
-   **Mixed online:** all classes will be remote, with some meetings at scheduled days and times and some asynchronous work
-   **Hyflex:** enrolled in synchronous online instruction, meeting at scheduled days and times

_**Please note that camera use may be required for synchronous and/or mixed online classes.

## Day 1 Textbook Access

CSULB offers [Day 1 Textbook Access](https://www.csulb.edu/beach-shops/day-1-textbook-access "Day 1 Textbook Access") (D1TA), a program that allows students to access all their required textbooks for a flat price.

Students may elect to opt out of Day 1 Textbook Access by the [deadline to add or drop classes without approval](https://www.csulb.edu/enrollment-services/key-dates-and-deadlines "Key Dates and Deadlines "). Prior to the deadline, students will receive email reminders about the program and the option to opt out.

Visit the [Day 1 Textbook Access website](https://www.csulb.edu/beach-shops/day-1-textbook-access "Day 1 Textbook Access") for more information.

After you enroll, continue to check your [MyCSULB Student Center](https://my.csulb.edu/) for class schedule updates. Hybrid and face-to-face classes require students to be on campus or community site.

**Select a term below to view the course schedule.** You can view offerings by Academic Subject, College, or General Education Category by using the buttons at the top of each page. Course lists are updated regularly to reflect the latest changes in the official schedule.

### SB 1359 – Zero Cost Course Materials

California Community Colleges and California State Universities now include a no cost symbol in the online campus course schedule that exclusively use digital course materials that are free of charge to students and therefore not required to be purchased. Please look for the no cost icon  

 next to your selected course indicating there is a no cost digital alternative available.
