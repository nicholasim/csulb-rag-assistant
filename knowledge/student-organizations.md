# Student Organizations

One of the best opportunities to become an active part of the campus community is by meeting new people, gaining leadership experience, and furthering your educational or extracurricular interests by joining a student organization. **CSULB has more than 300 recognized student organizations, representing 12 diverse categories:**

[Search Student Organizations on BeachSync](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/)

**What is BeachSync?** - BeachSync is CSULB's online community. On it you can interact with student organizations and stay up to date on campus events. Student organizations are required to registered on BeachSync, so you can always be sure that what is online is up to date.
