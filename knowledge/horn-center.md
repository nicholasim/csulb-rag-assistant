## The Horn Center

The Horn Center benefits students by ensuring open access to amenities such as a wi-fi enabled study lounge, multiple adaptive (height-adjustable) computer workstations, self-service print/copy machines, and an open computer lab. 

## Services and Spaces

-   [Technology Help Desk](https://www.csulb.edu/thd) provides assistance on personal computing and software issues, and campus data systems.
-   Self-service stations for [printing](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/how-print)
-   10 Active Learning Classrooms w/ lecture capture / Hyflex technologies

-   2 large lecture halls with lecture capture and in-class learning glass systems

-   Luxor locker system for classroom equipment checkout (for faculty)
-   Outdoor seating area

## Open Computer Lab

The University Open Computer Lab in the Horn Center ensures equal access to high-quality equipment, software, and onsite technical assistance needed for academic success and creative expression. The lab is open to all CSULB students, regardless of degree program or area of study and provides:

-   Computer Lab Helpdesk
-   226+ seats
-   42 PC workstations
-   12 M1 iMac computers
-   60 M4 Mac Mini
-   18 laptop docking stations
-   10 collaborative study areas for group assignments
-   MS Office and Adobe Creative Cloud software in all computers
-   Color, B&W, and wireless printing
-   ADA (adaptive) workstations with height-adjustable tables, large print keyboards, and lighted book magnifier units.
-   Computer peripherals available for checkout: headphones, keyboards, mice, HDMI, HDMI adaptors
     

### Online Exam Spaces

Students can reserve a space to take online exams at the the Open Computer Lab. To reserve space, please make an appointment:

[Book an Appointment](https://outlook.office365.com/book/HornCenterTestingCenter@csulb.onmicrosoft.com/)

### Features

-   Computers are equipped with [Respondus Lockdown Monitor](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-technology-services/instructional-design/respondus-tools).
-   Computer lab desks include individual dividers.

For questions about this resource, contact Derek Williams ([derek.williams@csulb.edu](mailto:derek.williams@csulb.edu "mailto:derek.williams@csulb.edu")), Open Lab Manager.
