## Veterans Services

Welcome to Veterans Services, CSULB's veteran and military-affiliated student support center. We are committed to supporting service members, veterans, and eligible dependents in transitioning to campus student life.

We help students access the educational benefits they earned by serving in the United States armed forces. To do so, we certify their enrollment in courses for the G.I. Bill® and process CalVet Fee Waivers for eligible dependents.

Additionally, we provide prospective students with information about applying for admission to CSULB.

The links on the left side of this page should guide you to most of the information you need to get started on the road to getting a degree from CSULB, so take some time reviewing the information we've compiled for you.

The links on the left side of this page provide information for prospective students, newly admitted students, and continuing students. If you are new to CSULB and plan to use VA education benefits, start by reviewing the Veterans Services Orientation information.

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.
