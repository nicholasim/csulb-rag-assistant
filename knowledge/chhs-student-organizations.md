## Sidebar

##

**What is BeachSync?**

[BeachSync](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/exerciseismedicinecsulb) is CSULB's online community. On BeachSync you can interact with student organizations and stay up to date on-campus events. Student organizations are required to register on [BeachSync](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/exerciseismedicinecsulb), so you can get up-to-date information on meetings, events, and activities.
