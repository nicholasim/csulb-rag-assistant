# Pharmacy

## ​Pharmacy Hours

**Mondays:** 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
**Tuesdays:** 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
**Wednesdays:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
**Thursdays:** 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
**Fridays:** 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Pharmacy closed for lunch from 12:30pm - 1:30pm.

**Over-The-Counters (OTCs) at SHS pharmacy are for Students, Faculty and Staff**

CSULB students, faculty and staff can purchase Over-The-Counter (OTC) items at reduced prices at the SHS Pharmacy. To purchase OTC items simply visit the pharmacy counter or [you may order our most popular OTCs online by accessing the "SHS Pharmacy" Tile in SSO](https://myapps.microsoft.com/) and pick up at the pharmacy during our business hours. 

Please be aware that there will be limited to two of any single item and 12 items total for each visit. 

**Prescription service at SHS pharmacy is for CSULB Students Only.**

-   Our pharmacy is located on site for the convenience of our students
-   We dispense prescriptions that are on our Drug Formulary List.
-   If you prefer, your prescriptions can be filled at an off-campus pharmacy.
-   We do not accept insurance plans for prescriptions in our clinic.
-   We do not accept prescriptions from outside providers at our pharmacy.
-   Prescription medications are available at reduced prices.

**Prices may change at any time. Our prescriptions medications are mostly in generic.**
