# Campus Resources

California State University, Long Beach is committed to the academic and personal success of all students including those who are pregnant and parenting. We have various support services designed to help pregnant and parenting students succeed and thrive at the Beach.

Please check out some of these resources and services available to student parents.

* * *

[**ASI Government**](https://www.asicsulb.org/corporate/government/executive-branch/cabinet-docs)

ASI Student Government supports pregnant and parenting students through a dedicated cabinet commissioner who advises the president on issues, advocates for policy changes, and provides programming tailored to this community's needs

[**Basic Need**](https://csulb.edu/basicneeds)

We support student success by addressing basic needs, offering resources like food, housing, financial wellness, and assistance with CalFresh and CalWORKs to help you thrive academically and navigate college life confidently.

[**BMAC**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/bob-murphy-access-center/title-ix-pregnant-and-parenting-students "Title IX: Pregnant and Parenting Students")

BMAC provides accommodations for student parents, these include medically necessary modifications, lactation breaks, and leave for childbirth i.e. 2 weeks for uncomplicated births and 6 - 8 weeks for C-sections to support academic success.

[**Child and Family Center**](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-health-human-services/child-and-family-center)

The Center is an educational program which provides training of early childhood professionals in Child Development and Family Studies. The Center also provides quality childcare services for CSULB employees and their families and opening services for student parents. Expected opening 2025-2026 academic year.

[**CAPS**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/counseling-and-psychological-services)

CAPS provides comprehensive support for students navigating personal challenges and mental health concerns. Services include short-term individual counseling, group counseling, case management, referrals to external resources, psychoeducational workshops, and immediate crisis intervention.

[**Isabel Patterson Child Development Center**](https://www.asicsulb.org/corporate/discover/child-development-center)

The Isabel Patterson Child Development Center offers affordable on-campus child care for CSULB student-parents, providing quality early care and education for 230 children, from 6 months to 2nd grade, with licensed and accredited staff.

[**Library**](https://csulb.libguides.com/childrenscollection/FamilyFriendly)

The Family Friendly Study Area in the library is a “no-shushing” area with children’s books, toys, and 2 children’s game computers where parents can come study and use library resources with their children in tow.

[**Office of Equity and Compliance**](https://www.csulb.edu/equity-compliance)

The Office of Equity & Compliance reviews concern under the CSU Nondiscrimination Policy. This includes discrimination on the basis of sex - including pregnancy, parenting and all related conditions. 

[**Partners in Research and Outreach for Health**](https://csulb-gurizar-prohealth.com/)

PRO-Health helps connect pregnant families to free resources like classes, diapers, and Doula services through community partnerships. Their Rooting for Better Health project offers free health screenings to support healthy births and infant development.

[**Student Life Development**](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/ppsa)

Student Life and Development hosts student organizations like Students with Dependents, which supports CSULB students in parenthood, guardianship, or pregnancy by offering events, resources, and advocating for nontraditional students' challenges with administration and faculty.

[**Leadership Resource Center**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/student-life-development/lois-j-swanson-leadership-resource-center "Lois J. Swanson Leadership Resource Center")

The Lois J. Swanson Leadership Resource Center offers two free programs for CSULB students: the Emerging Leaders Certificate, introducing leadership topics through workshops, and the Leadership Academy Certificate, enhancing leadership competencies through a structured curriculum.

[**Dean of Students**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/dean-of-students/resources-for-student-parents)

The Dean of Students Office supports pregnant, postpartum, and parenting students by assisting with balancing academic responsibilities and parenthood, ensuring both academic success and well-being. We are here to support your journey.

[**Student Health Services**](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/student-health-services)

Student Health Services promotes academic success and wellness through affordable, high-quality care. Our collaborative team provides confidential, inclusive health services and education to support the diverse needs of the campus community.
