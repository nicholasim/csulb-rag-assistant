# Impaction

After an extensive review, California State University, Long Beach (CSULB) is modifying some of the Major Specific Requirements for transfer students. 

## Major Specific Degree Requirements Modification

**College of Health of Human Services**

Kinesiology, B.S. (Options in Exercise Science, Fitness, & Sport Psychology and Leadership) 

-   Include MATH 112B as one of the Statistics options in the Required Major Preparation Courses. New requirement will read “Take one of the following: STAT 108 or PSY 110 or HDEV 190 or SOC 170 or STAT 118 or MATH 112B or MATH 113”. 

**College of Liberal Arts**

Business Economics, B.A. 

-   Add MATH 119A as an option for completing the calculus requirement. New requirement reads “MATH 115 or MATH 119A or MATH 122”.  

Economics, B.S. 

-   Remove MATH 115 from options for completing the calculus requirement and include MATH 123. New requirement reads “MATH 122 or MATH 123”. 

**College of The Arts**

Cinematic Arts, B.A. 

-   Add three recommended major preparation courses: CINE 101, CINE 210, and CINE 220
