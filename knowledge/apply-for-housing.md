# Apply for Housing

Students are encouraged to apply for University Housing once they have been admitted to the university. Remember that University Housing is not guaranteed. Do not make assumptions or rely on information from other students or campus offices. Refer to [Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/university-housing-residential-life/incoming-students/frequently-asked-questions) for more information. Further questions can be directed to the [Student Housing Office](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/student-housing "Student Housing"). Thank you for your interest and we welcome you to The Beach!

[APPLY HERE](https://csulb.starrezhousing.com/StarRezPortalX/Login)

## 2026-2027 Academic Year Housing Application

-   **Current Residents:** Defined as students who are currently residing on campus with University Housing
-   **Returning Applicants:** Defined as students who have previously been on-campus residents but are not currently in University Housing.
-   **New Applicants:** Refer to incoming freshmen and transfers, or any students who are applying for the first time with University Housing, regardless of class level.

## '26 - '27 New Applicants

##### New Application Window:

-   **Thursday, April 2nd at 10 AM PST - Friday, May 1st at 11:59 PM PST (Priority Deadline)**

##### How to Apply:

-   **Step 1** - You must be admitted to the university and have set-up a [MyCSULB account](https://csulb.okta.com/).
    -   You cannot apply for housing if you are currently waitlisted to CSULB.
-   **Step 2** \- Begin housing application on the Housing Portal (accessible through your MyCSULB SSO)
    -   Application will take 15-20 minutes to complete.
    -   Due to high demand, the application portal may not be available on your first attempt.
    -   Since applications are based on distance, students can apply any date and time between April 2 and priority date of May 1st.
    -   If you are applying before May 1st, the application is not first-come, first serve. If you are experiencing portal issues, try again later.
-   **Step 3** – Complete the Initial Payment of $275 along with the Non-Refundable Application fee of $50. Payment is required to complete the application.

##### New Applicant Housing Confirmation Process:

-   All applications received before **May 1st** will be prioritized based upon distance to campus. Student should be notified of housing confirmation or waitlist status by the second week of May.
-   There are approximately **3000 beds** for new students at CSULB. The 3000 students at the greatest distance from campus with completed applications by May 1st will receive housing priority.
-   If you apply by May 1st but our initial capacity has been reached due to the distance calculator, you may be placed on the waitlist. **All applications received after May 1st will be placed on a waitlist based upon date of application.**
-   As spaces become available throughout the summer and early Fall, students will be contacted and offered placement.

## '26 - '27 Current Resident Applicants

##### Current Resident Window:

-   **Tuesday, March 17th at 10 AM - Thursday, March 19th at 11:59 PM PST.**
-   Students complete the online application through the Housing Portal (accessible through your [MyCSULB SSO](https://sso.csulb.edu/))
-   A $275 Initial Payment along with a $50 Non-Refundable Application fee is required to complete the application.

##### Current Resident Housing Confirmation Process:

-   All completed applications will be randomized.
    -   The first 700 students in random generated list will be granted a space in housing.
-   All remaining students will be placed on a Paid Continuing waitlist based upon their order in the randomized system.
-   Paid Continuing waitlist confirmations are based on the number of Continuing cancellations.

## '26 - '27 Past Returning Resident Applicants

-   **Opens Monday, May 4th at 10 AM PST.**
-   Students complete the online application through the Housing Portal (accessible through your [MyCSULB SSO](https://sso.csulb.edu/))
-   A $275 Initial Payment along with a $50 Non-Refundable Application fee is required to complete the application.

All completed returning resident applicants’ applications will be placed on Paid waitlist by date of completion.
