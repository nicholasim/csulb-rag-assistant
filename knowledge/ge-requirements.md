# Fall 2025 GE Requirements

_Students who started attending a California Community College or California State University **in Fall 2025 or later** are encouraged to follow this GE program. Students will complete 34 lower-division GE units and 9 upper-division GE units._

**Reminder:** [Access your Academic Requirements Report (ARR)](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/academic-requirements-report-arr) to see your progress towards completing GE requirements. Continue to regularly connect with your academic advisor to plan your GE course selections.

## Can I change my GE pattern?

If you began your continuous enrollment at CSULB, another CSU, or a California Community College prior to Fall 2025, you may elect to change your GE pattern. Connect with your academic advisor for more information.

## Can I request a GE course substitution or waiver?

Some students may appeal to have earned credits applied towards their GE requirements. Always check with your academic advisor to see if an academic appeal is right for you. Examples of some appeals include:

-   CSULB GE Course Substitution
-   Transfer Course Substitution
-   GE Requirement Wavier

[Learn more about appeals and how to submit a request.](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/academic-standing-and-appeals)

## What is the GWAR?

To graduate, students must fulfill the Graduation Writing Assessment Requirement (GWAR) by successfully completing a Writing Intensive (WI) course with a grade of ‘C’ or better.  Students can find WI courses by selecting the "Graduation Writing Assessment" course attribute when they "Search for Classes" via MyCSULB Student Center. Students can also find a link to "GWAR Writing Intensive Courses" when viewing the [Schedule of Classes](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/schedule-of-classes "Schedule of Classes") "By GE Requirement" online for a specific term.
