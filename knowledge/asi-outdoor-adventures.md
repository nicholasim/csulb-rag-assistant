## Outdoor Adventures

Outdoor Adventure Trips are designed to give Long Beach State students fun and unforgettable experiences at various must-see destinations in the greater Southern California area. Activities include, but are not limited to, hiking, rock climbing, backpacking, surfing and paddleboarding.

Food, equipment and transportation are provided for all trips at no additional cost. Mandatory pre-trip meetings are required for all Overnight Trips.

For more information or to sign up for a trip, visit us in person at the Student Recreation & Wellness Center (SRWC) Rock Wall, or find us on the [**FusionGO**](https://csulb.innosoftfusion.com/Program?classificationId=e6eb4477-47d3-44ee-ac99-421b013690fc) app.

-   ### [Frequently Asked Questions](https://asirecreation.org/outdoor-adventures#)

    **When can I sign up for Outdoor Adventure Trips?**

    Trip registration begins during the first and second week of every semester. Registration for any specific trip ends on the Monday preceding the scheduled event. Check out [FusionGO](https://shop.asi.csulb.edu/Program/GetProducts?classification=e6eb4477-47d3-44ee-ac99-421b013690fc) for up-to-date information.

    * * *

    **How do I register for a Trip?**

    You can register online via the FusionGO app or by visiting the SRWC Rock Wall any day during hours of operation.

    * * *

    **I’ve just joined the waitlist for a trip. How will I know if a spot becomes available?**

    If a spot opens up for your trip, ROA will contact you via your student email address. Typically if you do not receive an email from us (1-week) before the trip date, we likely do not have any additional spots.

    * * *

    **How do I cancel?**

    Please contact us at [**This email address is being protected from spambots. We currently do not offer refunds, but if we can fill your spot with someone on the waitlist we will accommodate the refund.

    * * *

    **I’m on a trip, can I cancel and get a refund?**

    Currently, we do not offer refunds for our Adventure Trips. This is due to an overabundance of last minute cancellations in the past, resulting in the cancellation of entire Adventure Trips. However, if you notify us in advance, we will try to fill your spot with someone from the waitlist. If we are successful, we will refund you. Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy.

    * * *

    **What’s included in the cost of my trip?**

    ROA provides food, transportation, any technical equipment needed for the trip, and experienced, friendly guides!

    * * *

    **What do I need to bring for the trip?**

    Each trip has a trip-specific packing list. This can be found in the "Info Packet" that’s emailed to you the week of the trip. Any technical equipment/activity-specific equipment will be provided by ROA at no additional cost.

    * * *

    **What if I can’t come on the day of / week of the trip?**

    Please email us concerning your attendance regardless of how many days you have until the trip goes out. This way our trip leaders can plan accordingly. Also, this allows us to try and fill your spot with a new participant on the waitlist.
