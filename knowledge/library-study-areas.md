# Study Areas

To respond to customer demand for areas in which collaborative group study is permitted and accommodated, as well as for areas that are conducive to independent study free of distractions, signs are posted and building directories indicate as follows: 

**Group Study Areas:**

-   5th Floor
-   3rd Floor 
-   2nd Floor (Children's Collection is a Family Friendly area)
-   1st Floor Spidell Technology Center
-   Lower Level

**Quiet Study Areas:**

-   4th Floor
