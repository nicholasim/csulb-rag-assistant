# Make an Appointment

To make an appointment, please call Student Health Services at [562.985.4771](tel:+15629854771) during our clinic hours, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Wednesday 9 AM to 5 PM.

Available Health Services include:

-   Acute Illnesses and Infections
-   STI Testing and Counseling
-   Wellness Visits
-   Reproductive Health Exams
-   Pregnancy Tests and Pregnancy Options Counseling
-   Birth control options and prescriptions
-   Immunizations
-   Sports Medicine
-   Psychiatry
-   Smoking Cessation with free nicotine replacement
-   Dental Hygiene Services
-   Health Education Workshops
-   Family PACT

## Pharmacy Services

The SHS Pharmacy is open for service (prescriptions, refills, and over-the-counter items) from Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Wednesday 9 AM to 5 PM.

If you need a refill for an existing prescription (for example, birth control pills), but are no longer near campus, your refill can be transferred to an outside pharmacy.

Please call the SHS pharmacy at [(562) 985-7116](tel:+5629857116) and provide the following information:

-   Your Name
-   Student ID number
-   Cell phone number
-   Your preferred pharmacy and pharmacy phone number

A pharmacy staff member will transfer your existing prescription to your preferred pharmacy.

[Additional services](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/student-health-services/available-health-services "Available Health Services") including behavioral health, pregnancy options counseling, birth control education, STI counseling, smoking cessation, and substance abuse counseling are available.
