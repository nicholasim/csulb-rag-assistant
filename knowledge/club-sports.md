# Club Sports & Recreation

The Office of Club Sports & Recreation serves our student organizations that engage in more than 40 activities from Dance to Hiking to Water Polo. Our student-run groups are advised by a dedicated staff of advisors, and are assisted by a student staff that help to provide opportunities for activities day, night and on various weekends, both on and off campus.

With a wide variety of Club Sports & Recreation organizations at CSULB there is something for nearly everyone on campus - and no experience is necessary. Have interest in Ski & Snowboard, Surfing or Sailing but you've never tried it? No problem - our clubs are happy to show you the ropes. And if there's something you're interested in but it's not offered at CSULB, come talk to us and we'll discuss how to start a new Club Sport on campus. Find out more about the activities available through [Club Sports](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/club-sports-recreation/club-sports/club-sports).

The Office of Club Sports and Recreation strives to improve the quality of campus life for CSULB students and the campus community by providing a wide range of co-curricular activities through recreational and competitive clubs, educational programs and student organization leadership development.
