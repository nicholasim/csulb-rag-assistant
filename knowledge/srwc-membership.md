## Membership

Whether its fitness classes, personal training, rock climbing, intramurals, open recreation, meditation and relaxation, nutrition counseling or swimming, the Student Recreation & Wellness Center (SRWC) has something for everyone. We offer various membership categories to the Long Beach State community.

Registration is quick, easy and innovative! Instead of a membership card to gain entry into the SRWC, you will use our FusionGO App. Members will be able to log into their personal profiles to access all things rec-related, including a unique barcode which is used for contactless entry at the gates.

## Student Membership

Currently enrolled, matriculated, CSULB students, who are presently taking classes, are eligible and encouraged to use the Student Recreation and Wellness Center as their membership has already been paid through the University Student Union fee. In order to obtain membership and access to the facility, visit the SRWC, which is located near the Engineering Building on campus. Remember to bring your student ID!

Continuing students who are taking one semester off from school are encouraged to purchase a continuing student membership. The cost for this membership is $124 per semester and $94 in the summer. In order to receive this membership, students must bring a continuing education slip from Brotman Hall in order to verify their status. Students on academic leave may only purchase a continuing student membership one time in their history at CSULB.

**Summer membership:** Students enrolled in a summer session (excluding May Intercession) have access to the Student Recreation and Wellness Center through their summer tuition. Any non-graduating students not enrolled in summer courses, but were an enrolled student in the previous spring semester, and are returning in the fall, are eligible to purchase a summer membership at the prorated rate of $94 (first day of summer session through the last day of summer session).

_Continuing student memberships and summer memberships are non-refundable._

## CPaCE/ALI Students

CPaCE/ALI students Students in the College of Professional & Continuing Education (CPaCE formerly CPIE), American Language Institute (ALI) and Study Abroad @ The Beach (SA@B) are eligible to purchase a semester membership for $130, a rolling monthly membership for $37 a month, or a 30 day membership for $42. These membership options are only valid during the time of the student’s enrollment. Please visit the Student Recreation & Wellness Center with your student ID to sign up.

_Memberships are non-refundable and you must commit to a minimum of two full months for all rolling monthly memberships._

## Associates: Faculty/Staff/Alumni

Associates are regarded as full and part time University faculty and staff, auxiliary staff (Foundation, Chancellor’s Office, Carpenter Center and Beach Shops), alumni, volunteers, and retired or emeritus faculty or staff. Memberships are available at the prorated rate of $407 annually, rolling monthly membership for $37 a month, or a 30 day membership for $42.

**Staff and Faculty:** Staff and faculty members must present a valid campus ID at the front desk when signing up for a membership.

**Alumni:** Alumni members must present proof of alumni status prior to enrolling in a membership. All alumni are members of the Alumni Association and can access a digital membership card through the CSULB Alumni website, which may be used as proof. Alumni also receive a 10% discount on annual memberships.

**Volunteers:** Volunteers who have University documentation that demonstrate contract status of a minimum of one year are eligible for an associate rolling monthly membership of $37 per month.

**Lunchtime memberships:** Special rolling monthly rate at $15 per month. Grants access to the facility from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., Monday through Friday.

_Memberships are non-refundable and you must commit to a minimum of two full months for all rolling monthly memberships._

## Affiliates: Family/Donors/Summer Student

**Family:** Affiliates are spouse and legal domestic partners, parents, adult children, and adult siblings of a student or associate member. Affiliates must be accompanied by a currently enrolled and active student, or an active associate member to obtain membership.

Affiliate memberships are available at the rate of $462 annually or rolling monthly rate of $42 per month.

**Donors:** Donors who have given at least $5000 in a lifetime to the SRWC or ASI are eligible for an affiliate rolling monthly membership of $42 per month. Please contact the Membership Services and Outreach Coordinator at 562-985-7427 for more information.

**Summer Student:** Any student attending a U.S. college or university in the previous spring semester that brings a copy of their spring schedule. They must be accompanied by a currently enrolled CSULB student member at the time of purchase. Once the purchase and verification are complete, the summer student affiliate can visit the facility by themselves during the specified period.

Summer student affiliate memberships are available at the pro-rated fee of $150 (first day of summer session through the last day of summer session).

_Memberships are non-refundable and you must commit to a minimum of two full months for all rolling monthly memberships._

## Guest Passes

Guest passes are available to guests of SRWC members for $10 a visit. The guest must complete a liability waiver on the day of admittance, show photo ID verifying they are over 18, and must be accompanied by the sponsor for the duration of the visit. Guests are allowed to utilize the facility, except for taking part in Intramurals, Instructional classes, or renting out equipment. Members may sponsor up to two people at a time.

Continuing students who are not currently enrolled in school, but were enrolled in the previous semester and who are enrolled in the upcoming semester may purchase a $10 guest pass for themselves, instead of purchasing a full membership. However, these students cannot sponsor a guest.

_Guest passes are non-refundable._
