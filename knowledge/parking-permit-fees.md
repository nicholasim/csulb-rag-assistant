# Permit Fee Schedule (2026-27)

**Fee Freeze (2026-27):** Permit prices will not increase for Academic Year 2026-27.

After this 1-year fee freeze, parking fees will continue to follow the stated fee schedule, increasing at the rate of inflation as reported by the [Consumer Price Index (CPI)](https://dof.ca.gov/forecasting/economics/economic-indicators/inflation/) or by 5%, whichever is lower. This will enable the department to keep pace with the increased costs of material and labor that result from inflation and directly affect our operational expenses. The department continues to review its financial status and will make adjustments as necessary.

For more information about sustainable transportation alternatives to driving to campus, please visit [Sustainable Transportation](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/sustainable-transportation).
