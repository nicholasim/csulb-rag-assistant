# Clubs

The College of Liberal Arts is home to dozens of the most vibrant and active student organizations on campus.  

The American Indian Student Council puts on the [annual CSULB Pow Wow at Puvungna](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/student-life-development/pow-wow), the largest and oldest student-sponsored event on campus; the [Model United Nations](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-liberal-arts/political-science/about-model-united-nations) club gives students opportunities to collectively tackle the world’s most pressing issues; and the [Psychology Transfer Student Association](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-liberal-arts/psychology/psychology-transfer-student-association) organizes events to help students maximize academic success, research, career planning, and networking. 

These are only a few examples of the depth and breadth of student activities available; whatever your passion, there is a club or activity in the CLA to join.  

See the list below or visit the [CSULB Events & Orgs](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organizations?categories=11146) site for more information.
