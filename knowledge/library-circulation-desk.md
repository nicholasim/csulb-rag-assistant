# Circulation Desk

The Circulation Desk is located near the Library entrance. Go here to:

-   Register to borrow library materials (be sure to bring your current University ID card).
-   Check out and return library materials.
-   Renew materials if allowed. (See grid below. If renewal is allowed, renew online by logging into "[My Account](https://csu-lb.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/account?vid=01CALS_ULB:01CALS_ULB&section=overview)" in OneSearch.)
-   Inquire about books you were unable to locate on the shelves ([Search / Item Not on Shelf](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/search-items-not-shelf "Search / Items Not on Shelf")).
-   Inquire about lost personal items (Lost and Found).

### Borrowing Terms for eBooks

Reading availability and borrowing ability varies depending upon the license purchased. Some ebooks may only be read in a web browser via the provider’s web platform. Others may be downloaded and read offline using [Adobe Digital Editions](https://www.adobe.com/solutions/ebook/digital-editions/download.html) for loans of 48 hours or 21 days. Reading and borrowing of ebooks is available to current CSULB students, faculty, and staff. All other user groups may only access ebooks in a web browser from the on-campus network; offline/download options are not available for community borrowers.

Entire ebooks are not obtainable through Interlibrary Services; two chapters from an ebook may be requested via CSU+.

You may use [OneSearch](https://csu-lb.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/search?vid=01CALS_ULB:01CALS_ULB&lang=en&mode=advanced), the CSULB Library catalog, to log in to "[My Account](https://csu-lb.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/account?vid=01CALS_ULB:01CALS_ULB&section=overview)" and Renew Books. When viewing "[My Account](https://csu-lb.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/account?vid=01CALS_ULB:01CALS_ULB&section=overview)" you can determine what materials are checked out to you, renew items checked out to you, and see what materials are on hold for you. If you need materials not available at CSULB, [you have several options](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/borrow-another-library "Borrow from Another Library").

## Returning Library Materials - Book Drops

Borrowed materials (except those borrowed through CSU+) may be returned at any of the seven library book bins on campus. The map below shows the location of those book drops.

1.  Beach Drive across from Residence Housing (RH2)
2.  University Music Center Area
3.  State and East Campus Drive Turnaround (near the Science Building)
4.  South Turnaround between Education and Macintosh Buildings
5.  Two book drops at the Library entrance

If you have questions about borrowing or returning materials or [library fines or fees and how to make payments](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/fees-policy "Fees Policy"), please call [(562) 985-5512](tel:+1-562-985-5512).
