## Student Life & Development

Welcome to Student Life & Development (SLD). We’re here to help you make the most of life beyond the classroom by helping you explore exciting opportunities, tap into valuable resources, and build meaningful relationships that will shape your journey at California State University, Long Beach. Get involved, create unforgettable experiences, and make your time truly extraordinary at _The Beach_!

One of the best opportunities to become an active part of the campus community is by meeting new people, gaining leadership experience, and furthering your educational or extracurricular interests by joining a student organization. **CSULB has more than 300 recognized student organizations, representing 12 diverse categories.**

### Mission:

To provide co-curricular opportunities for student engagement, leadership development, and personal growth.

### Additional Resources & Information

## Benefits of Being Involved

-   Make social connections and find a sense of community.
-   Make a positive difference on campus and in the community.
-   Develop transferable leadership skills.
-   Interact with faculty, alumni, business and community leaders and experts in various disciplines.
-   Increase the likelihood that you will succeed in achieving your academic degree.
-   Gain exposure in career fields by collaborating with professionals and staff.
-   Enhance your resume with demonstrated skills valued by potential employers.
