# Housing

Explore our housing resources tailored to assist students in securing safe and stable housing options. 

* * *

## **Emergency Housing**

-   Short-term emergency housing, up to 21 days support. 
-   Eviction avoidance grants for students who received a 3-day notice or eviction notice. 
-   Deposit and first month rent grants are available for students who are experiencing homelessness and have located housing. Documentation from landlord of move-in costs are required. 
-   [Speak with a case manager](https://cm.maxient.com/reportingform.php?CSULongBeach&layout_id=2) about Emergency Housing.  

* * *

## **Rapid Rehousing**

-   Rapid- rehousing support for sudden loss or unsafe housing; students will work with CSULB funded community partners to locate new housing and receive assistance with lease process and deposit. 
-   [Speak with a case manager](https://cm.maxient.com/reportingform.php?CSULongBeach&layout_id=2) about Rapid Rehousing
