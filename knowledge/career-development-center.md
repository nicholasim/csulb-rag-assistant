The CSULB Career Development Center empowers students to become effective leaders of their personal career journeys. Through career counseling, employer connections, and professional development opportunities, we prepare them to become skilled and productive members of the local, national, and global workforce.

## Make an Appointment

Schedule a [career counseling appointment through Beach Connect](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center/students/career-counseling-appointments "Career Counseling Appointments") on topics including major and career exploration, resume review, job search strategy and more.

## Find a Job

Looking for an [internship, on-campus job, or part-time/full-time position](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center/students/job-internship-search "Job & Internship Search")? Use CareerLINK to find positions with employers who want to hire from the Beach!

## Resumes & Cover Letters

Find [tips to make your resume and cover letter](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center/students/resumes-cover-letters "Resumes & Cover Letters") stand out and view sample resumes specific to your college.

## Workshops & Events

[Workshops, job fairs, information sessions and more](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center/students/student-events-workshops "Student Events & Workshops")! Check out the latest happenings at the CDC and how to attend.

## Post a Job

Employers, learn how to [promote jobs or internships](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center/employers/employer-programs-services "Employer Programs & Services") to CSULB students and alumni on CareerLINK.
