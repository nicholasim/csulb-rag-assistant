## **Our Mission** 

The LGBTQ+ Resource Center at California State University, Long Beach is committed to fostering a vibrant, inclusive, and affirming campus environment for students, staff, and faculty of all sexual, romantic, and gender identities. Through intersectional advocacy, holistic support services, and transformative education, we empower individuals to explore and celebrate their identities, build community, and engage in social justice. We strive to challenge systems of oppression, promote equity, and cultivate a culture of care, resilience, and belonging across the CSULB community.

* * *

_All students, regardless of identity, who are interested in the mission and focus of the LGBTQ+ Resource Center, are welcome to visit and spend time at the resource center and participate in any programs and events._
