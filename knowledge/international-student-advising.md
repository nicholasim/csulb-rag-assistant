# Meet with an Advisor

**International Student and Scholar Advising Services**
Are you a current international student or visiting scholar (researchers and professors) at CSULB?  Please follow the instructions below to drop in or schedule an appointment with an Advisor. 

**Note:** Scheduled appointments are conducted in person or over Zoom. Drop in advising is done exclusively over Zoom. If you select a Zoom appointment, or join a drop in advising session, please make sure you have access to a computer with an enabled microphone and camera.  [Learn more about using Zoom.](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-technology-services/instructional-design/zoom)
 

## Schedule an Appointment

Use [Beach Connect](https://sso.csulb.edu/) to schedule an appointment with an International Student Advisor.

-   [How to Book Appointments \[Video\]](https://youtu.be/TFKf3XNz5Lg)
-   [Make an Appointment with an Advisor](https://www.csulb.edu/sites/default/files/2023/documents/CIE_MakeAppointmentWithAdvisor%202023.pdf "CIE_MakeAppointmentWithAdvisor 2023.pdf") (PDF)

## Drop-In Advising

**NOTE:** Drop-in Zoom advising is for **current students or students on OPT** (Optional Practical Training).

Drop-in advising is a short 5-10 minute session over Zoom. Advisors are unable to provide a more in-depth advising appointment. Please be mindful of other students who are waiting to be advised.

**Hours:**

-   Mondays:    9:00am - 11:00am
-   Tuesdays:   1:30pm - 3:30pm
-   Wednesdays: 9:30am - 11:30am
-   Thursdays:  1:30pm - 3:30pm

_Drop in advising will not take place: November 11th, November 17th, November 26th, November 27th_ 

_The office is closed during all campus_ [_academic holidays_](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-affairs/academic-affairs-calendar)_._ 

**How to Join Zoom**
For online video drop-in advising sessions: when you enter the Zoom meeting during the designated times, you will be added to an online queue. Please do not leave the Zoom meeting, just wait until the advisor starts your session.

-   [Click here to attend a Zoom meeting​](https://csulb.zoom.us/s/89070672089) (ID: 890 7067 2089)

## J-1 Visiting Researchers & Professors

Email Mr. Ignacio Castor for an appointment: [Ignacio.Castor@csulb.edu](mailto:Ignacio.Castor@csulb.edu)

-   **After making an appointment:**
    An International Student Advisor will send you a Zoom meeting invitation via email. The Zoom meeting will be scheduled for your appointment date and time.
     
-   **On the day and time of your appointment:**
    Use the meeting invitation email from your advisor to log in to your Zoom session. If you are a current student, use Zoom in MyCSULB single sign-on to add the meeting ID.
