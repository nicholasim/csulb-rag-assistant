# Student Organizations

Being a part of one or more of these organzations can broaden your network and expand the resources available to you.

## Society of Physics Students

_BeachSync link:_ [_Society of Physics Students (SPS)_](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/society-of-physics-students-sps)

The Society of Physics Students (SPS) is a professional association explicitly designed for students. Membership, through collegiate chapters, is open to anyone interested in physics. The only requirement for membership is that you be interested in physics. Besides physics majors, our members include majors in chemistry, computer science, engineering, geology, mathematics, medicine, and other fields.

[National website: Society of Physics Students](https://www.spsnational.org/)

## Women in Physics

_BeachSync link:_ [_Women in Physics_](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/wip)

This organization aims to build community and support gender diversity among physics students at CSULB. Transgender women, cisgender women, non-binary people, and gender diverse physics students are all welcome in Women in Physics (WiP)! The goal is to promote a sense of belonging for each person in our physics community, as well as provide an opportunity to network and learn from each other about academic and career development while navigating the field as an underrepresented gender.

## Astronomy Club

_BeachSync link:_ [_Astronomy Club_](https://csulb.campuslabs.com/engage/organization/astronomy-club)

The Astronomy Club aims to ignite an interest in all the universe offers beyond Earth's atmosphere through community, club, and volunteering events. Join for more astronomical updates and future activities!
