# Tuition and Fees

Ready to embark on your academic journey at Cal State Long Beach? **Explore the resources below to learn more about fees, deadlines, methods of payment, and more!**

**Reminder:** All students enrolled at a CSU campus pay a systemwide tuition fee, in addition to campus mandatory fees. The CSU makes every effort to keep student costs to a minimum. All listed fees, other than mandatory system-wide fees, are subject to change without notice, until the date when instruction for a particular semester begins. All CSU listed fees should be regarded as **estimates that are subject to change** upon approval by the Board of Trustees, Chancellor, or the President, as appropriate.

## Non-Resident Tuition

If you are **not a resident of California**, _you must add $471.00 for each unit of credit  you take_ to your tuition total.

**Example Non-Resident Tuition for an undergraduate who wishes to register for 12 units:**

-   Total California Resident Tuition and Mandatory Fees: $4,365.00
-   Multiply the number of units (12) by $471.00 = $5,652.00
-   Add the two amounts together for total non-resident tuition: $4,365.00 + $5,652.00 = $10,017.00

Students may be exempt from non-resident classification if they are a teacher, military service member, California high school graduate who has attended a California high school at least three years, or a dependent child of California residents. If so, you must apply for an exemption every semester through Enrollment Services.

[**Learn more via Residency for Tuition Purposes.**](https://www.csulb.edu/admissions/residency-for-tuition-purposes)

## Non-Resident Tuition

If you are **not a resident of California**, _you must add $471.00 for each unit of credit  you take_ to your tuition total.

**Example Non-Resident Tuition for an undergraduate who wishes to register for 12 units:**

-   Total California Resident Tuition and Mandatory Fees: $4,365.00
-   Multiply the number of units (12) by $471.00 = $5,652.00
-   Add the two amounts together for total non-resident tuition: $4,365.00 + $5,652.00 = $10,017.00

Students may be exempt from non-resident classification if they are a teacher, military service member, California high school graduate who has attended a California high school at least three years, or a dependent child of California residents. If so, you must apply for an exemption every semester through Enrollment Services.

[**Learn more via Residency for Tuition Purposes.**](https://www.csulb.edu/admissions/residency-for-tuition-purposes)

## About Cost of Attendance

Tuition and fees represent only a part of your total education costs, which include items such as books, supplies, and living expenses. At Cal State Long Beach, the following elements are included in standard Cost of Attendance estimates:  

-   Tuition and Mandatory Fees
-   Living Expenses (Housing and Food)
-   Books, Course Materials, Supplies, and Equipment
-   Transportation
-   Miscellaneous Personal Expenses
-   Federal Student Loan Fees 

Except for tuition, mandatory fees, and on-campus housing, all other components of Cost of Attendance are based on the data from the Student Expenses and Resources Survey administered by the California Student Aid Commission.  

**View your program's full cost of attendance and additional details** [**via Cost of Attendance.**](https://www.csulb.edu/costs)   

## Understanding CSULB Mandatory Fees

#### _Supporting Your Student Experience, Success, and Campus Life_

Your mandatory fees at CSULB help fund essential services, programs, and facilities that support your academic journey and enhance campus life. Here’s how your fees work for you:

**Health Facility Fee:** Supports the construction, maintenance, and operations of the Student Health Center, ensuring you have access to a safe, modern space for your healthcare needs.

**Associated Students, Inc. (ASI) Fee:** Funds Associated Students, Inc. (ASI), a nonprofit organization that enhances student life through programs, services, events, facilities, and student-led initiatives across campus. For more information, please visit the [ASI](https://www.asicsulb.org/corporate/resources/about-us) website.

**Instructionally Related Activities (IRA) Fee:** Provides funding for hands-on learning experiences beyond the classroom, including academic competitions, performances, research opportunities, athletics, and support programs tied to your coursework.

**Student Recreation & Wellness Center (SRWC) & University Student Union (USU) Fee:** Supports the construction, operations, services, and ongoing improvements of campus facilities where you can stay active, connect with others, and access resources. This includes recreation programs, student events, study spaces, and wellness services. For more information, please visit the [Student Recreation & Wellness Center](https://www.asirecreation.org/) and [University Student Union](https://www.asicsulb.org/corporate/resources/usu-directory) websites.

**Student ID Card Fee:** Supports the operation of ID Card Services which issues the official CSULB student ID card. This ID card provides access to various campus services, programs, and resources. For more information, please visit the [Beach Shops](https://www.csulb.edu/beach-shops/id-card-services) website.

**Student Excellence Fee:** Invests in your success by supporting academic advising, technology upgrades, cultural and resource centers, campus transportation, and athletics—enhancing your overall student experience.

**Student Health Center Fee:** Funds a wide range of health, wellness, and preventative services to help you stay healthy and succeed. For more information about the services offered, please visit the [Student Health Services](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/student-health-services) website.
