## The City of Long Beach

One of the many reasons CSULB is a great place to live and learn is our location. Explore the [City of Long Beach](http://www.longbeach.gov/), featuring the historic Queen Mary, the Aquarium of the Pacific's exhibits, the Shoreline Village boardwalk and much more. Discover the diverse and thriving coastal community that is the City of Long Beach.
