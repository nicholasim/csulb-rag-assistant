# About Fraternities And Sororities

Fraternity and Sorority Life is an integral part of the CSU Long Beach campus as well as throughout the Long Beach community and beyond. Fraternity and Sorority Life was founded on Scholarship & Learning, Leadership & Values, Philanthropy & Service, and Brotherhood & Sisterhood. Being part of these organizations will provide ample opportunities for our student leaders to participate in activities that will enhance their personal development, promote academics success, and advance leadership skills. The university is committed to the positive development and creating an inclusive learning environment for our Greek-letter affiliated students.

Please use our website to learn more information about our community whether you are a current member, prospective member, parent, advisor or a resident of the Long Beach community.

If you have any comments for us to consider or would like to communicate with us directly, please connect with us at [FraternityandSororityLife@csulb.edu](mailto:FraternityandSororityLife@csulb.edu).
