## What does my 'Apply to Graduate' fee pay for and can it be refunded if I do not participate?

This non-refundable fee helps cover the costs associated with the final preparation for graduation and the awarding of a student’s degree. 

### The fee, set by the university, includes the following services:

-   Commencement ceremonies
-   Degree audit and evaluation by advisors throughout the year
-   Creating and mailing each student’s diploma

Refunds are not given as the fee pays for services already performed. Currently, there are no waivers for the _Apply to Graduate_ fee.

For a full description of the process and the fee, please go to: [Student Records - Apply to Graduate- What happens after I Apply to Graduate.](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/apply-to-graduate-undergraduate#:~:text=When%20Do%20I%20Apply%20to)  Full dates, deadlines, and fee schedules can be found on the Enrollment Services page [here](https://www.csulb.edu/enrollment-services/key-dates-and-deadlines).

## Which term do I choose when Applying to Graduate?

When you _Apply to Graduate_, select your last term of study. For instance, if your final term before completing your degree is Fall 2026, choose Fall 2026 from the dropdown menu. Do not select Spring 2027 as your graduation term if you are only participating in the Spring 2027 ceremonies, as the ceremony is not directly tied to your graduation term.

If you submit your application after the deadline, there is no assurance that your name will be included in the online graduate recognition program since the deadline is March 1st. Additionally, make sure to contact [commencement@csulb.edu](mailto:commencement@csulb.edu) to request inclusion, providing your name, ID, college, and major, and we will add you to future communications.

## Why is it important to Apply to Graduate by March 1 or at all?

To be included in the official commencement program and have access to the RSVP platform, you must complete the _Apply to Graduate_ process. This adds you to our commencement database, granting you access to RSVP in your student portal. We use this information for name reading, stage display, and inclusion in your college's Graduate Recognition Program.

If you miss the _Apply to Graduate_ deadline (March 1), contact [commencement@csulb.edu](mailto:commencement@csulb.edu) to request inclusion into the RSVP portal, providing your name, ID, college, and major, and we will add you to future communications. 

Students who do not record their name pronunciation before **the Commencement RSVP portal closes on April 17** will still have their name read aloud by a name reader as you they cross the stage, but name or degree information will **not be displayed** on screen. You also won't be included in the printed Graduate Recognition Program (though we can add you after June 1).

**Commencement RSVP Portal will open mid March and close on April 17.** Due to the process of program finalization, name recording, display loading, and creating of the Grad Pass, information changes or additions are no longer possible after April 17. Please RSVP through SSO by April 17 to ensure you're fully recognized. 

_Applying to Graduate_ is the process to obtain your official degree; commencement is the _celebration_. These are separate processes. You can participate in the ceremony without officially graduating if you still have academic requirements. 

## How do I RSVP to my ceremony and get my TWELVE (12) guest tickets ?

If you have completed the **Apply to Graduate** process (discussed above), the RSVP tile will be visible in your SSO dashboard mid March. Through the RSVP platform, you can confirm your attendance, record your name pronunciation, and receive your guest tickets code.

## How do I know if I am graduating with honors?

Honors based on GPA are not acknowledged during the ceremony since GPAs are computed only after the degree has been officially conferred, typically several weeks after the semester concludes. If you have already graduated before the ceremony and are aware of your final GPA, you have the option to buy an honor cord at the [University Bookstore](https://www.fortyninershops.net/site_grad.asp). Additionally, you can indicate your honor level through personalized graduation announcements, which are also available at the Bookstore.

For inquiries regarding honors specific to your major, please reach out to your [college coordinator](https://www.csulb.edu/commencement/commencement/form/questions-and-suggestions-for-csulb-commencement).

## What if I need to push back my graduation term? Will I need to apply to graduate and pay again?

If you need to change your graduation term, please reach out to your academic advisor. You will NOT need to reapply to graduate nor pay the fee again if you already paid. You do NOT need to let us know.

## **General**

## What is the difference between "graduation" and "commencement"?

**Commencement** serves as the _celebratory event_ dedicated to acknowledging students who have completed their degree or are in the process of doing so. Our office specifically manages the coordination of Commencement celebrations and ceremonies.

Please be aware that CSULB organizes annual commencement ceremonies at the conclusion of the spring semester for students meeting their degree requirements. There is currently no fall commencement ceremony. Summer and fall graduates have the option to participate in the May commencement ceremony either before or after completing their degree. To do so, they must apply for graduation via **MyCSULB Student Center** ([link](https://www.csulb.edu/enrollment-services/using-mycsulb-personal-information#:~:text=Setting%20Your%20Diploma%20Name&text=In%20MyCSULB%20Student%20Center%2C%20under,the%20bottom%20of%20the%20page.)) in the spring semester of the year they plan to participate.

For questions about **commencement ceremonies**, please contact [commencement@csulb.edu](mailto:commencement@csulb.edu).

**Graduation** _fulfills your degree requirements_. Taking part in the commencement ceremony does not imply or guarantee graduation or the receipt of honors. 

[_**Enrollment Services**_](https://www.csulb.edu/enrollment-services)manages all aspects related to _graduation, eligibility, degree checks, diplomas, changing name on diploma, class credit, grades, name and address changes, adding/removing FERPA (Family Education Rights and Privacy Act), and graduation status._ 

For inquiries regarding **fees,** **degree progress, or graduation matters**, please reach out to Enrollment Services at [es-registration@csulb.edu](mailto:es-registration@csulb.edu).

Children **2 years old and younger** do **NOT** need a ticket, and **MUST** sit in a guest's lap.

## Parking Information

For detailed parking information please visit linked [Driving & Parking at Angel Stadium](https://www.mlb.com/angels/ballpark/transportation/directions) page. ALL PARKING IS FREE!

## Service animals vs emotional support animals (ESA) at the ceremonies.

No pets except service animals are allowed. The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) defines a service animal as a dog that is individually trained to do work or perform tasks for a person with a disability. Dogs or therapy animals whose function is to provide comfort or emotional support _do not qualify as service animals._ 

_**Service dogs must be under the handler's control at all times.**_ 

**Animal Relief Areas:**
Please have your service dog relieve itself outside the stadium prior to the ceremony. If your service dog needs relief during the event, kindly exit the stadium and use areas outside the immediate venue, out of consideration for other guests and nearby food concessions. Please clean up after your dog and dispose into one of the many nearby trash receptacles.

Please refer to our page on ACCESSIBILITY AND ACCOMMODATIONS, or contact [BMAC@csulb.edu](mailto:BMAC@csulb.edu) if you have any questions.  

## Are posters, noisemakers, or confetti allowed?

Noisemakers and confetti are NOT allowed.

Per policy, all banners and signs brought into the venue must meet the following guidelines: 

-   Must be in good taste (as determined by CSULB/Angel Stadium staff personnel).
-   Cannot be commercial, political or obscene in nature.
-   May not be affixed to any wall, staff, or pole.
-   Cannot impede entry or exit into aisles or seating, and are NOT ALLOWED in the graduate processional line.
-   Faces on sticks are acceptable.
-   You may not bring anything in that obstructs the view of other guests or poses a safety risk to anyone (i.e banners, flags, umbrellas, large/tall hats).

## How do I request accommodation for the venue?

Please fill out the following form for [Disability-Related Accommodations](https://csulb.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_9QrCZIMm0KNtYzj "Commencement 2024: Request for Disability-Related Accommodations") (link) by **May 1**.

## Stage Access Policy

Only graduating students are permitted on stage during the ceremony. Approved service animals are also allowed. _Family members and individuals who are not graduating, including children, are **not** permitted on stage._

This policy is in place for safety and operational reasons. Graduates often wait in line for an extended period in warm conditions, which can be difficult for children. The stage environment also presents potential safety risks, including tripping hazards, limited space, and the possibility of individuals wandering or becoming separated. In the event of an emergency, the stage and surrounding areas must be cleared quickly and efficiently.

We appreciate your understanding in helping us maintain a safe and smooth ceremony experience for all participants.

View the linked [Angel Stadium Seating Map](https://www.mlb.com/angels/tickets/seating-map) & linked [Angel Stadium Driving and Parking directions](https://www.mlb.com/angels/ballpark/transportation/directions) for more information.
