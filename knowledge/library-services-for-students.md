# Services for Students

## Borrowing / Lending Services

## Identification Cards

Your CSULB campus ID card serves as your library card. You must have a valid CSULB campus ID in order to use some library materials and equipment. Students should call [(562) 985-7388](tel:+15629857388) for additional information regarding photo ID cards.

## Borrowing Terms

The basic loan period for books is 16 weeks. The loan period for media materials (audio cassettes, compact discs, videos, and DVDs) is 30 days. Periodicals and reference materials are not available for check-out.

All student accounts expire before the beginning of the next semester following graduation. 

Students who otherwise separate from the University will no longer have library access. 

## Renewals

Books may not be renewed.  You will receive a reminder notice by email prior to the due date.

## Fees & Fines

Bills for all fees are sent by the University Business Office via mail or email. Payments can be made on myCSULB or at the Cashier's Office in Brotman Hall. Please report lost materials to the Circulation Desk. Lost book fees include replacement costs and processing assessments. 

## Other Services

## Computer Labs

[The Spidell Technology Center](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/computer-labs-at-the-university-library "Computer Labs at the University Library"), 1st Floor, is one of two Open Access Computer Labs at CSULB. Over 200 computers are available in the Library building.

(562) 985-7820

## Photocopy Services

Photocopiers are located on the Lower Level and the 1st Floor. You can use your Campus ID card to pay for photocopies in any of the campus copy centers. Courtesy copier cards may be purchased at the Value Transfer stations on the Lower Level and 1st Floor. Copying or printing in the Library is 10¢ per page.

(562) 985-4754

## Periodicals / Microform Materials and Equipment

Latest issues of periodicals are available in unbound format in Current Periodicals behind the Research and Information Desk. Microform materials (microfilms, microfiche, microcards) are available by request from the circulation desk and the equipment to read and print such materials are also behind the Research and Information Desk.

(562) 985-4028

## Special Collections and University Archives

Unusual and valuable collections of rare and fragile books, original art prints and photographs, and historical papers are located in [Special Collections](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/welcome-to-special-collections-and-university-archives "Special Collections and University Archives") on the 3rd Floor.

(562) 985-4087

## Services for Persons with Disabilities

We are committed to providing accessibility of library materials, programs, and services to all students. Staff working in public areas will make every effort to accommodate requests from library users whose disabilities limit their use of the Library's facilities and resources. If services are needed beyond those listed please contact Library Administration at (562) 985-4047.

[Services for Persons with Disabilities](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/services-for-persons-disabilities-0 "Services for Persons with Disabilities")

## Delivery Options for Distance Learners

Distance learners should request materials (books, articles, conference proceedings, etc.) not available at CSULB using BeachReach, with these cautions: 

-   Complete the appropriate [BeachReach](https://www.csulb.edu/university-library/beachreach-faqs "BeachReach FAQS") Interlibrary Loan form. Distance learners need to indicate their status every time they make a BeachReach request by noting answering YES to the question on each form "Are you are distance learner?"
-   As a special service to distance learners, the CSULB Library will send through snail mail any materials (including those owned by CSULB) that can not be delivered electronically (this generally applies to books).
-   For books, the date due and the return address will be provided you when you receive the book. You are responsible for the payment of return postage.
-   If available electronically, journal articles will be emailed to you. If no electronic copy is available, a photocopy will be sent to you by first class mail.
-   If the library owns the item you request and it is on the shelf, it should arrive in less than a week. Items that must be requested from other libraries will take longer than CSULB owned materials. (Check [OneSearch](https://csu-lb.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/search?vid=01CALS_ULB:01CALS_ULB&lang=en&mode=advanced) to see if CSULB Library owns the item you want.)
-   Check on the progress of your request by logging in to [BeachReach](https://illiad.library.csulb.edu/BeachReachLogon/) Interlibrary Services and "Review Status of Requests and Retrieve Electronically Delivered Items".
-   Contact Circulation for more help 562.985.5512
