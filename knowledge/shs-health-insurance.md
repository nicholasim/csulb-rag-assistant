# Health Insurance

The Student Health Services provides basic care for acute medical conditions, injuries, and illnesses, as well as support services such as Behavioral Health, Psychiatry, Case Management, Laboratory, Pharmacy, X-ray, Gender Affirming care, Sports Medicine, and Wellness programs. Most care and services are free of charge with the exception of the pharmacy, laboratory, and immunization clinic.

All students enrolled for the current semester have paid a $90.00 Student Health Fee included with tuition, which provides access to the Student Health Services. Please note that the Student Health Fee is not health insurance and Student Health Services does not bill or accept insurance.

If your income is $1400/month or less and your parents do not claim you on their taxes, you may be eligible for Medi-Cal at no cost to you. 

-   **Apply online** at [Covered California website](https://www.coveredca.com/), or
-   **Apply in person** at the Long Beach Health Department at 2525 Grand Ave, Room 219, Long Beach, CA 90815; call [562.570.7979](tel:+15625707979) for an appointment.
-   **If you are currently enrolled in Medi-Cal in another county**, you will need to change your Medi-Cal to LA County to access local providers; call your home county case worker to update your address. Our Case Manager can assist with any Medi-Cal questions.

If you currently have health insurance but do not know how to find a clinician that is covered under your health plan, or you need assistance purchasing health insurance, you can schedule an appointment with our Referral Nurse by calling Student Health Services at [562.985.4771](tel:+15629854771).

If you are an international student seeking health insurance coverage, please visit [the CSULB International Students website](https://www.csulb.edu/international/current-students/health-insurance) to find out more about the current insurance options and pricing.
