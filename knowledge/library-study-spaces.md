# Find Your Seat on Campus

Looking for a place to study or relax? ASI repurposed furniture from the USU and relocated it to various spots across campus. This page includes those relocated spaces as well as a comprehensive list of all indoor and outdoor seating available across campus. To explore these locations visually, visit our [**interactive campus map**](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ct/91380,91382,91383,99695?s/)

For all the night owls out there, visit the ASI After-Hours Study Center located in the [**Hillside Dining Hall**](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/87276?ct/91380,91382,91383?m/1100933?s/hillside?z/19).
