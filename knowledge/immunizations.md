## CSULB Student Immunizations

The CSU Immunization Policy ([Executive Order 803 dated February 2023](https://www.csulb.edu/sites/default/files/2023/documents/ExecutiveOrder803_4-14-23.pdf)) requires students to obtain the vaccinations and screening recommended for colleges and universities by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH) for those diseases that can be passed on to others by respiratory transmission.

Executive Order 803 provides for a university or university-sponsored program to implement additional discretionary requirements in accordance with CDPH guidelines and following consultation with the Office of the Chancellor.

At California State University, Long Beach, we believe the health of the individual can affect the health of the entire campus community. We are committed to protecting the health and well-being of our students, the campus community, and beyond to allow students to focus on educational and personal goals.

Student Health Services is enrolled in the California Immunization Registry or CAIR. CAIR is a secure, confidential, statewide computerized immunization information system for California residents. The registry is accessed online to help providers and other authorized users track patient immunization records, reduce missed opportunities, and help fully immunize Californians of all ages. Please click the [CAIR disclosure policy](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CID/DCDC/CAIR/Pages/CAIR-updates-disclosure.aspx "CAIR discloser policy") for more information.

## Sidebar

#### **How to Comply with the CSULB Immunization Requirements:**

 All new Spring and Fall 2026 CSULB students are **REQUIRED** to provide proof of vaccination per Executive Order 803. Please upload your records to the [Patient Portal](https://csulbportal.pointnclick.com/). 

**Please note: Records may take 7 - 10 business days to process after they have been submitted to the Patient Portal.**

1.  Please allow 7 - 10 days for vaccine records to be updated in your Patient Portal.
2.  Be prepared! Gather all of the information and documents you'll need to complete your requirements online.
3.  Log in to the [Patient Portal](https://csulbportal.pointnclick.com/). Please note: you must pay the $150 enrollment deposit in order to be able to upload documents to the Patient Portal. Without the $150 enrollment deposit, you have access to the Patient Portal, but cannot upload the required documents. [Click here for information on how to submit your payment and click on the drop down "Accept Your Admission Offer."](https://www.csulb.edu/admissions/freshmen-application-process#:~:text=In%20addition%20to%20submitting%20documents,in%20the%20upcoming%20Fall%20class.) **Note: the $150 enrollment deposit does not apply to Continuing or Graduate students.**
4.  Upload images or PDF files of your immunization records or laboratory tests to show proof of immunity (titers). Please note that titers will only be accepted for MMR, Varicella, and Hepatitis B (if applicable) .If you have both an immunization and titer, please upload the immunization record only, not both the immunization and the titer.
5.  Complete the Tuberculosis Risk Screening in the [Patient Portal](https://csulbportal.pointnclick.com/).
