# Federal Work-Study

Federal Work-Study (FWS) is a type of financial aid that allows students to earn money through part-time employment on the CSULB campus. Students must be enrolled in six units and offered FWS in their financial aid in order to work under this program.

The intent of the program is to assist students with paying for educational expenses. FWS jobs are available during the fall and spring semesters to undergraduate, credential and graduate students who meet federal and institutional eligibility requirements.

Being eligible for FWS does not guarantee you a FWS position. Even if you're awarded work-study, you must still apply for and secure a position through your school’s job placement process. Please note, there are usually more non-FWS jobs available than FWS jobs.

We encourage students to join an upcoming workshop or review the recording and presentation slides, posted below, for details on the FWS process.

## Students

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I maintain my eligibility?

-   Complete your Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) each academic year by March 2.
-   Receive FWS award offer in your financial aid award
-   Enroll in minimum of 6 units per semester
-   Maintain good academic standing according to [Satisfactory Academic Progress](https://www.csulb.edu/financial-aid/understanding-satisfactory-academic-progress-sap) standards
-   Maintain Office of Financial Aid eligibility criteria

## How do I look for jobs?

You can explore all available, on-campus **Experiential Learning (Federal Work Study)** positions on CareerLINK through your [Single Sign-On](https://csulb.okta.com/). Each posting includes the job duties, hourly rate, and specific application instructions provided by the hiring department. All applications must be submitted through CareerLINK, following the steps outlined in the individual job posting. Departments will review applications in CareerLINK and contact selected candidates directly to schedule interviews.

## How will I create a resume if I do not have work experience?

Reflect on activities you participated in at school, as a volunteer, or during community service. You can include any required skills or work responsibilities used acquired during those activities. Contact the [Career Development Center](http://www.careers.csulb.edu/ "Career Development Center") for assistance with your resume.

## How will I be paid?

You will be paid monthly with a pay date of the 15^th of each month. You can sign up for direct deposit through the CSULB Payroll department or receive a paper check from your FWS supervisor.

## What is the money that is on my financial aid labeled as "Federal Work-Study"?

This is the maximum amount you can earn from a Federal Work-Study job for the academic year.

## How much can I earn?

You can earn up to your FWS award amount listed in your [MyCSULB](https://csulb.okta.com/) account. The department that you are working for will set your hourly rate of pay, which will be at minimum the California State minimum wage. You and your supervisor will determine your schedule, up to a maximum of 20 hours per week, during the academic year.

Please note: FWS award amounts are subject to change before and during the school year, depending on your eligibility and other financial aid awards. You and your supervisor will be notified if changes are made to your FWS award.  

## Is the Federal Work-Study amount pre- or post-tax?

The amount of Federal Work-Study funding listed on your financial aid is pre-tax dollars. Contact Human Resources, Payroll Department at 562-985-4164 or at [payroll@csulb.edu](mailto:payroll@csulb.edu) for details.

## Who determines the hourly wage for Federal Work-Study?

Hourly wages are determined by the skills and qualifications for the job and department budget limitations. Discuss the hourly pay rate at the time an offer is made.

## Can I sign up for payroll direct deposit at any time?

Yes, you may sign up at any time, but it will take 1-2 months to go into effect. Sign up on the MyBeach app. Contact Payroll Services at [payroll@csulb.edu](mailto:payroll@csulb.edu) or 562-985-4164 for details.

## Is e-refund the same as direct deposit from Federal Work-Study payroll?

Federal Work-Study payroll direct deposit is not the same as direct deposit from financial aid/student accounts. Human Resources will provide you the direct deposit form with your new hire documents. Contact Payroll Services at [payroll@csulb.edu](mailto:payroll@csulb.edu) or 562-985-4164 for details.

## Who should I contact if I have to change my work schedule or I am not able to come to work?

Contact your immediate supervisor for changes to your schedule, time sheet, or any other concerns related to your job.

## Will Federal Work-Study affect receiving scholarships or other aid?

There is a possibility that additional funding, such as scholarships, can affect your FWS amount. [Contact Financial Aid and Scholarships](https://www.csulb.edu/financial-aid-and-scholarships/contact-financial-aid-and-scholarships "Contact Financial Aid and Scholarships") for details.

## Will using my Federal Work-Study affect my family income?

One of the benefits of Federal Work-Study earnings is that the money you earn will not affect your family income on your FAFSA. [Schedule an appointment](https://www.csulb.edu/enrollment-services/join-the-queue-financial-aid "Schedule appointment with Financial Aid and Scholarships") with our Financial Aid and Scholarship counselors for assistance on how to report earnings on your financial aid application.

## Is Veterans Work-Study the same as Federal Work-Study?

No, Veterans Work-Study is different from Federal Work-Study. [Contact Veterans Services](https://web.csulb.edu/divisions/students/veterans_university/ "Veterans Services") on campus for more details.

## Supervisors

## How do Federal Work-Study (FWS) students maintain their eligibility?

Students must:

-   Complete their FAFSA each academic year by the State Priority Deadline (usually March 2nd)
-   Receive FWS award offer in their financial aid award
-   Enroll in minimum of 6 units per semester
-   Maintain good academic standing according to [Satisfactory Academic Progress](https://www.csulb.edu/financial-aid/understanding-satisfactory-academic-progress-sap) standards
-   Maintain Office of Financial Aid eligibility criteria

## How will I know if my FWS student is eligible for my position?

Verify with your potential candidates that they are offered a FWS award by requesting a copy of their financial aid awards from their MyCSULB account.

Please note: FWS awards may change at any point during the academic year. If a FWS student’s award changes, the student and employer will be notified via email.

## How do I hire a FWS student?

-   The ASM notifies the FA Office Federal Work Study (FWS) Team that job(s) have been created.  This is done using the shared workbook your ASM maintains.  

    Once your Position Request Form is approved through the Office of Financial Aid, you can post your open positions on CareerLINK

    -   If you have questions about this process please contact the FA FWS Team at [es-work-study@csulb.edu](mailto:es-work-study@csulb.edu)
-   Post your position via CareerLINK using the following steps:
    -   [Visit the Career Center website](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center)
    -   Click on the Employers CareerLINK Button

    -   Sign In and Post Job OR “Sign Up and Post Job” if you are a first-time user

              For new registrants: “Company or Organization Name,” use this format: “CSULB - Department Name”

              For returning users: Please select “Post to this school only”

    -   IMPORTANT: Must first select Position Type: Experiential Learning Type (FWS) so new form will appear

              Select Experiential Learning Placement Type: Federal Work Study

              Select Work Term: “Fall 2024” or “Fall 2024 - Spring 2025”

              Complete the form, and click on “Submit”

    -   Career Center staff will review your form(s) and email you about your account and job posting approval

    -   Contact the [Career Development Center](https://www.csulb.edu/career-development-center) for questions on posting your position

-   Interview your potential employees
    -   FWS is considered part-time employment at CSULB. The Office of Financial Aid highly encourages employers to conduct interviews with potential candidates to ensure the student is a good fit for the department
-   Once you have selected your employee, contact your ASM for what forms and documentation must be submitted
