# Residential Dining Services

**All residents are required to select a meal plan to live in University Housing.**

[Residential Dining](https://www.csulb.edu/49er-shops-at-the-beach/residential-dining) offers a variety of meal plan choices to fit the needs of our residents. Each plan can be used at any of our three residential dining halls. Residents "swipe" their Student ID Card each time they enter any residential dining facility during the posted operating hours of each location. The Dining Halls' food service is all-you-can-eat buffet style.

## MEAL PLANS

**19 Meal Plan**

-   $5,898 per academic year
-   19 meals per week at any residential dining hall
-   Unused meals at the end of the week do not roll over to the next week
-   Unused meals are not refundable
-   Residents may enter the dining hall once per meal period

**210 Block Plan**

-   $5,560 per academic year
-   210 meals per semester
-   Unused meals at the end of the semester do not roll over to the next semester
-   Unused meals are not refundable

**10 Meal Plan**

-   $5,080 per academic year
-   10 meals per week at any residential dining hall
-   Unused meals at the end of the week do not roll over to the next week
-   Unused meals are not refundable
-   Residents may enter any dining facility once per meal period

_Rates are subject to change._
