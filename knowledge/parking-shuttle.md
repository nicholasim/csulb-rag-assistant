# Shuttle

Campus shuttles are provided by Parking and Transportation Services for free to all students, staff, and faculty. Please show your campus ID card to board the campus shuttle. 

Shuttles do not operate on days when classes are not in session (fall break, spring break, winter break, and other federal holidays). Full schedule, hours and route information is provided below and also on the free [Passio GO!](https://csulb.passiogo.com/) app.

Shuttle arrival times are approximations based on averages. Similar to city buses, these times may fluctuate based on the amount of vehicle traffic on campus, rain or other inclement weather, and ridership volume as additional time is often needed to board large groups of students or individuals requiring ADA assistance.

Additional information is provided below.

## Beachside

This shuttle is designed to transport Beachside residents to/from the Beachside campus to the main CSULB campus. Non-Beachside students may ride this shuttle if space is available, but priority seating will be given to the Beachside residents with the Beachside sticker affixed to their ID card.

The Beachside shuttle runs at the following times and frequencies:

### Mon.-Thurs., 6:30 a.m.-7 p.m.

Picks up approximately every 15 min.\*

### Mon.-Thurs., 7 p.m.-11 p.m.

## Shuttle Code of Conduct

CSULB aims to provide safe, reliable, and efficient transportation for students, faculty, and staff. The Code of Conduct outlines the expectations for behavior of passengers to ensure a safe and respectful environment for all.

### Code of Conduct

1.  Please treat drivers and fellow passengers with respect and courtesy.
2.  Always allow passengers to exit the shuttle before boarding the shuttle.
3.  Open containers of food or drinks are not allowed on campus shuttles. Food and beverage containers must remain sealed at all times to prevent spills and maintain shuttle cleanliness for riders.
4.  Keep personal belongings secure. The university is not responsible for lost, misplaced, or stolen items.
5.  Remain seated at all times when the shuttle is in motion.
6.  No animals are allowed on campus shuttles except for trained service animals.

For any additional questions, please email [ride@csulb.edu](mailto:ride@csulb.edu).
