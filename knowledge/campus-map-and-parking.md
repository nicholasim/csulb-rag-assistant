# Campus Map and Parking

Parking at CSULB is available for $15 a day at any of the “G” parking lots indicated on the map. We suggest parking in lot G3, G4, G5, G6 or the Pyramid Parking Structure, as they are the closest lots to the College of Business (COB), Horn Center (HC), Anatol Center (in AS), and University Theater (UT).

Parking can be paid through

-   [The parking mobile app](https://www.csulb.edu/parking-and-transportation-services/article/parkmobile-app)
-   [Parking pay stations](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1314#!ce/37105?ct/63337,45996,44104,41975,55572,55573,44735?m/447568?sbc/)
