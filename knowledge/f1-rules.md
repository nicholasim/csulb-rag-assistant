# F-1 Rules

All F-1 international students must follow U.S. immigration regulations to maintain valid student status at CSULB. Because federal rules change rapidly, please use the resources below to stay compliant and find up-to-date guidance:

-   [**View Immigration News & Updates**](https://www.csulb.edu/international/current-students/news-and-updates): Check this section regularly for recent policy changes and urgent regulatory alerts.
     
-   [**Meet with an ISS Advisor**](https://www.csulb.edu/international/current-students/meet-an-advisor)**:** Connect with an advisor during our scheduled drop-in hours for personalized assistance with your status.

Review the essential rules below to keep your F-1 status on track.

## Full Course of Study

International students must be registered for a full course of study at CSULB during Fall and Spring semesters. Enrollment for summer and winter sessions is optional.   

**Required Units Fall/Spring:**

-   [Bachelor's and Credential Students](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/student-unit-load) - 12 units
-   [Master's Students - 9 units (8 units is considered full-time at CSULB)](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/student-unit-load)
-   We encourage all students to take more than the minimum required and/or enroll in summer break to be able to complete their program by their I-20 end date.

**Online Courses:**

-   Only 3 units or one online course per semester can be applied to the full-time enrollment total.
-   International students cannot register in _all_ online courses during their final term.
-   For students sponsored by their home country's government or organizations, there may be additional limitations. Please consult with an International Student Advisor before enrolling in online courses.

**Enrollment Below Full-time Units**
In special, limited circumstances, enrollment below a full course of study may be approved (see the section below). [Meet with an International Student Advisor](https://www.csulb.edu/node/560046) to discuss any circumstances that may impact your ability to enroll in a full course of study.

## Enrollment Below a Full Course of Study

To be enrolled below full-time, students need authorization from an [International Student Advisor](https://www.csulb.edu/node/560046) prior to the beginning of each semester. 

Students who are enrolled for less than full-time without authorization will be in jeopardy of losing their F-1 student status and will need to resolve the status issue in order to legally continue their studies in the U.S.

**Possible Exceptions**
The following is a list of possible reasons for enrolling below a full course of study (Note: the reasons listed are not a guarantee for approval):

-   Academic difficulty
    -   Please meet with an [International Student Advisor](https://www.csulb.edu/node/560046)
-   Illness or medical condition
    -   Please meet with an [International Student Advisor](https://www.csulb.edu/node/560046)
-   Final semester of study with less than full-time units remaining to graduate:
    -   _Undergrads Only_: For undergrad students enrolled in under 12 units in their final semester, the course schedule must be final at the time of request submission. [Submit a request for a full course exception](https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/Requests/ServiceDet?ID=51797).
    -   _Graduates Only_: For a post-baccalaureate student enrolled in final units, research, graduate projects, and/or comprehensive exams, the course schedule must be final at the time of request submission. [Submit a request for full course exception](https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/Requests/ServiceDet?ID=51797).
         

Student will need to request an extension of stay if a reduced course load will delay their time to completion of studies.

## Maintaining Valid Immigration Documents

**I-94**

As an F-1 student, you must maintain a valid I-94 Arrival/Departure Record showing your admission in F-1 status with "D/S" (Duration of Status), and carry a printed copy with your passport and I-20 at all times. After each entry into the U.S., download your most recent I-94 from the [CBP I-94 website](https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/), verify the status and date information is correct, and save a printed copy for your records.

**I-20**

International students must be aware of the expiration date on their I-20. If students cannot complete their degree by the Program End Date on their I-20, students must make an appointment with an International Student Advisor to request a program extension as soon as possible. The process of reviewing an extension request may take some time and must be completed prior to the program end date on the I-20 form. Failure to obtain a program extension within the time limit allowed will result in the cancellation of your F-1 student immigration status. See the Program Extension section below for more information.

**Passport**

Students should keep their passports valid at all times. If the passport will expire within 6 months, students must renew it through the embassy or consulate of their home country. All international students must provide an updated copy of their passports to their respective international program offices.

## Changing Majors

Students who wish to change majors must speak with their program advisors for permission. 

_New Rule Postponed_

_**Undergraduate Level Students:** F-1 undergraduate level students are not allowed to change programs or majors during the first year of their program (including transfers)._

_Those who wish to change major must first meet with an_ [_International Student Advisor_](https://www.csulb.edu/node/560046) _for approval. If approved from the school, the students must undergo an application filing process with USCIS along with fees and the I-20 from CIE. A change in major may also require an extension of program._

_**Graduate Level Students:** F-1 graduate level students are not allowed to change programs or majors._

## Program Extension

F-1 students who are unable to complete their degree programs prior to the program end date on their I-20 form (under the Program of Study section on Page 1) must make an appointment with an [International Student Advisor](https://www.csulb.edu/node/560046) prior to the expiration of the I-20.

Reasons for possible considerations of program extensions: 

-   Medical
-   Academic

The process of reviewing an extension request may take some time and must be completed prior to the program end date on the I-20 form. 

Failure to obtain a program extension within the time limit allowed will result in the cancellation of your non-immigrant F-1 student status.

_New Rule Postponed_

_All extensions require that the student file an I-539 to be reviewed directly with USCIS. Extension requests are not guaranteed._ 

## Change of Local Address

International students who change their U.S. address must report the change to CSULB within 10 days of moving by changing your mailing address in your [MyCSULB](https://sso.csulb.edu/) account.

## Grace Period

F-1 students who complete an academic program or OPT have a 60-day grace period and J-1 students who complete an academic program have a 30-day grace period. During the grace period, students need to leave the U.S., gain a new F-1 status, or seek another immigration status.

F-1 students who have received prior authorization from an international student advisor to withdraw from classes have 15 days to depart the U.S. after the withdrawal date. Students who withdraw from classes without prior authorization are not eligible for a grace period.

_New Rule Postponed_

_The grace period will be reduced from 60 days to 30 days._

## Educational Leave - Academic Regulation

International degree-seeking students wishing to file for an educational leave of absence during a fall or spring term must meet with an International Student Advisor for an appointment to discuss important F-1 student status and university policies in detail. We recommend that you plan ahead and meet with an international student advisor to gather more information before filing a leave of absence at CSULB.

### According to CSULB regulations:

Any registered student, undergraduate or graduate, in good academic standing may request an Educational Leave. Students requesting an Educational Leave must complete an Educational Leave Form, in advance, including an explanation of their reasons for seeking the leave and a statement of when they intend to resume academic work. The completed form should be submitted to the Office of Enrollment Services, Brotman Hall 101.

The minimum initial leave will be one full semester; the maximum will be one calendar year. A student may submit, in advance, a new educational leave request form for an extension of leave. Under no circumstances will the total number of approved educational leaves exceed two, nor will the duration of approved educational leaves extend beyond two calendar years.

Students returning from an approved educational leave are not required to submit an application for readmission to the university. Students returning from an absence for which an educational leave was appropriate but not approved in advance must reapply for admission and pay the reapplication fee.

Graduate students who plan to enroll for credit at another institution of higher education during the leave period must obtain prior approval for the transfer of course credit to the student's program from the department graduate advisor, department chair, and the College Dean or designee.

The period of educational leave is counted in the calculation of elapsed time under the regulations governing the maximum period for completion of degree and remediation requirements. ([See Baccalaureate and Graduate sections of this catalog](http://catalog.csulb.edu/).)

For the period of educational leave, the student's rights under the 'Election of Regulations' rule are preserved, maintaining the right of the student to elect regulations as if he or she had maintained continuous attendance.

An educational leave presupposes no expenditure of University resources during the period of the leave. No computer facilities, no library privileges, and no student services are available to a student on educational leave.

(CSULB University Catalog, 2015)
